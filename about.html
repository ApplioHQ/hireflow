Career tools that keep your experience yours.

Applio is a free AI resume builder and career toolkit for job seekers. It helps turn your real experience into a clear resume, tailor it to a role, check it against a job description, and prepare for what comes next.

Free by default

Every core feature is available without a credit card, subscription, or watermark.

Grounded in your input

AI suggestions are meant to clarify and organize your experience, not invent employers, credentials, or results.

Built for the full search

Use one workspace for resumes, ATS checks, cover letters, interview practice, job tracking, and career planning.

Clear about limits

Applio provides guidance and estimates, not hiring guarantees, legal advice, or a replacement for your own judgment.

Our editorial standard

Career advice should be specific enough to use and careful enough to trust. Applio prioritizes direct answers, examples, and links to original sources where a factual claim depends on outside research.

For an explanation of how Applio approaches resume scoring, AI suggestions, salary estimates, and sources, read our methodology and editorial policy.
