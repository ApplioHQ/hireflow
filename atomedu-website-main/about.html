About

About atomEDU

atomEDU makes Proof Lab: a free tool for showing math steps and checking whether they hold up. It is built around explicit rules and honest limits, not an AI chat pretending every answer is certain.

Why

Why it exists

An answer alone does not tell you where your reasoning changed course. We wanted a place to enter the math naturally, inspect a worked solution, and test the steps you wrote yourself.

Proof Lab separates notation, calculation, proof checking, and graphing. Ghost steps can reconstruct skipped moves, but they stay visibly distinct from the work you submitted.

Our approach

How we build it

Our math engine works through supported algebra and geometry families and checks results against its own rules and certificates. It can distinguish exact verification from numerical exploration or an unsupported request. We publish coverage inside the tool so that a gap is visible.

Proof Lab runs locally in your browser and is free to use. No account or paid tier is needed.

Who it's for

Who it is for

Students and educators who want to inspect algebra or geometry reasoning, not just copy a final answer. This is an evolving tool with bounded coverage. If a supported problem behaves incorrectly, or a rule you need is missing, tell us through the contact page.
