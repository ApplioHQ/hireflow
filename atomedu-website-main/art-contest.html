03 / THE FINE PRINT

Contest rules & terms

THE JUDGING GUIDE

How we choose a winner

We score eligible art out of 10 in each category, for up to 30 points. The highest-scoring entry is the daily winner. If there is another eligible entry, the second-highest scorer is the runner-up. Our owners review every score before publishing the results.

UP TO 10 POINTS

Beauty

How visually appealing and well put together the artwork is.

UP TO 10 POINTS

Creativity

How original and imaginative the idea is.

UP TO 10 POINTS

Complexity

How difficult and intricate the graph work is.

01

Use atomEDU’s graph

Only original artwork you create in atomEDU Proof Lab’s Graph view is eligible. Use its PNG save control and upload your saved image. Desmos graphs, art from other software, and work you did not create are disqualified, even if submitted in an accepted image format.

02

Enter as often as you like

You may make unlimited submissions each day while entries are open. Each submission contains one image. Every entry must meet all contest rules.

03

Protect people’s privacy

Do not include photos, faces, or other personal imagery. Do not put contact details, identifying information, or private information in the artwork or your display name.

04

Keep it appropriate

No inappropriate or NSFW content, including hateful, violent, sexually explicit, illegal, or harassing material. Such entries may be removed and may lead to a temporary or permanent ban from this and future contest rounds.

05

Age and permission

You must be at least 13 to enter. If you are under 18, you must have permission from a parent or guardian. We do not ask for your birth date. The first checkbox confirms these requirements and your agreement to the rules.

06

Ownership and display

You keep ownership of your work. You allow atomEDU’s owners to review your entry. Your separate display consent lets us publish your image and display name on this site and in the archive if selected as winner or runner-up.

07

Judges and decisions

atomEDU’s owners judge eligible entries. We add the Beauty, Creativity, and Complexity scores for each entry. The top scorer wins; the second-highest scorer is runner-up when there is a second entry. If any totals tie, the judges must review and resolve the scores before results can be published. The owners confirm the final results. Their decisions are final, with no appeals. Entering does not guarantee a feature or prize. We archive the featured entries and remove the other submissions for that day only after confirmation.
