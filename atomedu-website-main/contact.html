FAQ

A few quick answers

What math does Proof Lab handle?

Selected Algebra 1, Algebra 2, and geometry families. The current topic list is inside Proof Lab; a full curriculum is not guaranteed.

Does it cost anything?

No. Proof Lab is free to use in your browser, with no account or paid tier.

Can I suggest a rule or report a wrong step?

Yes. Email the problem and the step at issue. A reproducible example makes it easier to investigate.

Does the tool save my work?

Proof Lab does not provide an account or a saved progress history. Copy anything you want to keep before closing the page.
