Coverage

What it checks today

Selected Algebra 1, Algebra 2, Geometry, and Precalculus families are supported. That includes equations, inequalities, functions, systems, conics, coordinate geometry, exact unit-circle and vector rules, and explicit-premise geometry proofs. Coverage is rule-by-rule, not a promise to solve every textbook problem. Proof Lab lists its supported topics inside the tool.

See current coverage →

Built, not wrapped

Our engine does the mathematics.

Proof Lab is not a chat interface placed over somebody else's model. atomEDU built the parser, exact arithmetic, solver routes, step verifier, proof certificates, practice generator, and graphing workspace that power the product.

Each supported result must survive explicit mathematical rules and certificate replay. If the engine cannot verify a case, Proof Lab labels the limit instead of inventing an answer.

Connected by choice

Partners build trust. Integrations prove utility.

Official partners show that outside organizations are willing to work with atomEDU. Product integrations show that Proof Lab fits into the tools students and teachers already use.

The logos are supporting evidence, not borrowed credibility. Google, GitHub, Notion, Dropbox, and other integrations do not perform or certify the math. The verification engine remains atomEDU's own technology.

Questions, answered

What makes atomEDU different?

What is atomEDU?

atomEDU is the team behind Proof Lab, a free browser-based learning tool for solving supported math, checking student reasoning, generating practice, and exploring graphs.

Is atomEDU an AI wrapper?

No. Proof Lab uses atomEDU's own deterministic symbolic math engine. It parses the problem, applies explicit rules, tracks restrictions, and replays proof certificates before it displays a verified result.

What is Proof Lab?

Proof Lab is a math solver, step checker, practice generator, and graphing workspace for selected Algebra 1, Algebra 2, Geometry, and Precalculus topics.

Is Proof Lab free and private?

Yes. Proof Lab requires no account, and supported symbolic work runs directly in your browser. Work stays in the current session unless you choose to export or share it.
