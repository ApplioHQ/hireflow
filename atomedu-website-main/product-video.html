Why we built it

Verification

What “verified” means

The engine checks a result with a certificate tied to the original problem. Valid notation alone never earns the mathematical verified label.

Coverage

Supported rules, clearly listed

Proof Lab supports selected Algebra I, Algebra II, Geometry, and Precalculus families. Each family has its own tests and limits.

Boundaries

When the engine stops

Unsupported notation, unproved assumptions, and numerical pictures are labeled honestly. Students can still explore without mistaking that for a proof.
