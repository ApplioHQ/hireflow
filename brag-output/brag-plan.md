# Brag Plan: Applio

## What is this app?
Applio is a free AI resume builder that tailors resumes to any job description, scores them against ATS filters with a 0-100 score, and shows you exactly what to fix -- 31 templates, cover letters, interview prep, and a job tracker, all free with no credit card.

## The angle
The premium product film for a tool that costs nothing. The video treats Applio with the restraint and confidence of a paid SaaS launch -- elegant motion, clean type, slow reveals -- and then the punchline: free forever. The tension between the production quality and the price is the hook.

## Hook (first 2-3 seconds)
The Applio logo mark fades in on the dark background (#111113), followed by a single line: "The resume that gets you interviews." Slow, confident, no rush. The product speaks before anything moves.

## Key moments (the middle)
- The mock resume card from the hero -- the "Jane Doe" resume with the green "ATS Optimized" stamp and the 96/100 score ring filling up. This is the signature visual.
- A simulated tailoring moment: a job description text appears, then bullet points rewrite themselves to match. The AI doing its thing.
- The four scoring dimensions appearing one by one: Keywords, Formatting, Impact, Completeness -- cards arriving with quiet authority.

## Outro / punchline
"Free forever. No credit card." holds for a beat, then "appliohq.com" settles below. The restraint is the punchline -- this much polish for a free tool.

## User flow worth showing
Pick a template -> paste a job description -> AI rewrites bullets to match -> ATS score fills to 96/100 -> export clean PDF. The centerpiece scene shows the ATS score ring animating up while matched keywords highlight green.

## Tone
- Preset: polished
- Creative direction: quiet premium product film for a genuinely free tool
- Interpretation: Fewer scenes, longer holds. Confidence through slow reveals and generous whitespace. No excitement -- let the product earn it. Mixed case, light-to-medium weight type. Slow crossfades. The energy says "we don't need to convince you."

- Role: steady, clean music bed with restrained professional accents
- Music: happy-beats-business-moves-vol-12-by-ende-dot-app.mp3 (steady and clean, fits polished tone)
- Music treatment: start at 0s, volume 0.30-0.35, gentle fade-in over first 0.5s, fade-out over final 1.5s
- Music cue guidance: bundled preset at `<skill-dir>/assets/music/cues/happy-beats-business-moves-vol-12-by-ende-dot-app.music-cues.json`. Tempo ~110 BPM. Strong cues at ~8.74s (ATS score reveal), ~13.11s (scoring dimensions), ~18.56s (free forever punchline). Beat grid for sequential card reveals.
- Audio-reactive treatment: subtle; use music RMS/bass to make the accent glow and card surfaces breathe gently. No waveform/equalizer visuals.

- Audio-coupled moments: ATS score ring filling (soft success chime), scoring dimension cards arriving one by one (gentle card-place sounds), final logo (single soft bell)
- Restraint rule: audio must never compete with the visual calm. No aggressive hits, no dense layering.
