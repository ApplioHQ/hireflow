Rezi vs Teal: Honest Comparison (2026)

Updated 2026-09-05 · Applio Editorial
Rezi and Teal solve different problems. Rezi is an ATS-focused resume builder with strong keyword scoring. Teal is primarily a job tracker with a resume builder attached. If you're applying to many jobs and want to manage the whole pipeline, Teal fits. If you want the tightest ATS-optimized resume, Rezi fits. Both are usable free but require paid plans for the good stuff.

What Rezi is best at

Rezi is a focused ATS resume builder. Its core loop is: paste a job description, get a real-time ATS score, add keywords, watch the score go up. The scoring feedback is fast and quantified, and the templates are all single-column ATS-friendly by default.

Rezi is a good choice if:

  • You care about ATS optimization above all else
  • You want live scoring feedback while writing your resume
  • You're primarily building one strong resume for a target role
  • You don't need robust job tracking (Rezi's tracker is minimal)

What Teal is best at

Teal is a job-search operating system with a resume builder attached. Its strength is the Chrome extension that lets you save any job posting from any site in one click, then tracks status, next steps, and notes for each application. The resume builder integrates with the job tracker, letting you tailor a resume to a saved posting.

Teal is a good choice if:

  • You're actively applying to 5-50 jobs and want a real pipeline view
  • You want to track contacts and networking outreach alongside applications
  • You value the Chrome extension for saving jobs from LinkedIn, Indeed, etc.
  • You want cover-letter and email templates alongside the resume

Pricing (actual cost)

Rezi

  • Free: 1 resume, limited AI usage, basic templates
  • Pro: ~$29/month or ~$99/year (~$8.25/month equivalent)
  • Lifetime: ~$249 one-time (occasional promotions bring this lower)

Teal

  • Free: full job tracker, basic resume builder, unlimited saved jobs
  • Teal+ Weekly: ~$9/week
  • Teal+ Monthly: ~$29/month
  • Teal+ Quarterly: ~$79 (~$26/month equivalent)
  • Teal+ Annual: not consistently offered
Rezi is cheaper if you commit annually (~$99/year vs Teal's ~$316/year at monthly rate). Teal's weekly pricing is a good fit if you're between jobs and expect to be done in a few weeks.

ATS-friendliness

Both tools take ATS parsing seriously. All Rezi and Teal templates are single-column and use standard section headings that ATS software parses cleanly. Rezi's real-time ATS scoring is more granular; Teal's scoring appears once you tailor to a saved job.

Both are meaningful upgrades over Zety, Resume.io, or Canva if ATS-readiness is a concern.

AI writing quality

Both use large language models to suggest bullet phrasing and improve summaries. Neither is dramatically better than the other in isolation. Rezi's AI is tightly coupled to keyword suggestions; Teal's AI is more focused on tone and clarity.

Neither tool prevents you from AI-generating content that isn't grounded in your actual experience. The output quality depends heavily on the inputs you provide.

Which one should you pick?

Pick Rezi if: Your bottleneck is resume quality and ATS optimization for a specific role. You want to nail one strong resume rather than manage a big pipeline.

Pick Teal if: Your bottleneck is application volume and pipeline management. You're applying to many roles and losing track of which ones need follow-up.

Pick both if: You have the budget. They don't overlap fully. Some users pair them.

Pick neither if: You want a fully free solution, or you want ATS scoring plus job tracking plus AI resume writing without a monthly subscription.

Frequently asked questions

Which is better, Rezi or Teal?

They solve different problems. Rezi is a focused ATS resume builder; Teal is a job tracker with a resume builder attached. Pick based on which of those bottlenecks matters more.

Is Rezi free?

Rezi has a free plan (1 resume, limited AI). Full features require Pro at ~$29/month or ~$99/year.

Is Teal free?

Teal's job tracker and basic resume builder are free. AI resume writing and ATS scoring require Teal+ (~$29/month).

Does Rezi have a job tracker?

Yes but it's basic. Teal's tracker is significantly more feature-rich and central to that product.

Which has better ATS scoring, Rezi or Teal?

Rezi's real-time scoring is more granular and immediate. Teal's scoring is available on tailored resumes with Teal+. Both are meaningfully better than tools without built-in ATS scoring.
