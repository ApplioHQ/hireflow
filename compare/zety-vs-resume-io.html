Zety vs Resume.io: Honest Comparison (2026)

Updated 2026-09-05 · Applio Editorial
Zety and Resume.io are near-clones owned by the same parent company (BOLD). Both let you build a resume for free but charge to download it (roughly $2.95 trial that auto-renews to $24/month, or annual plans around $6/month). Feature parity is close to 100%. Pick whichever design you prefer. If you want a genuinely free download, use a different tool.
This is a neutral editorial comparison. Applio (the publisher of this page) is also a resume builder, we mention it briefly at the end. This page is not sponsored by either Zety or Resume.io and does not link to either as an affiliate.

Why they feel so similar

Zety and Resume.io are both owned by BOLD Ltd. (also known as BOLD Inc.), the same parent company that owns MyPerfectResume, LiveCareer, and several other career-services brands. The two tools share templates, infrastructure, business model (build-free, pay-to-download), and marketing playbook. Reviews of both tools are usually indistinguishable.

This isn't a secret, it's disclosed in Zety and Resume.io's respective legal pages, but most comparison articles online treat them as independent products and miss it.

Pricing (the actual cost)

Zety

  • 14-day trial: ~$2.95 (auto-renews to ~$23.95/month)
  • Monthly: ~$23.95/month
  • Annual: ~$71.40/year (~$5.95/month equivalent)

Resume.io

  • 7-day trial: ~$2.95 (auto-renews to ~$24.95/month)
  • Monthly: ~$24.95/month
  • Annual: ~$95.40/year (~$7.95/month equivalent)
Real-world cost: Most users who don't cancel the trial before it renews end up paying $23-$25 for a month. Read the terms carefully before signing up for either.

Templates and design

Zety: ~20 templates, most of which use multi-column layouts. Design leans corporate. Most template thumbnails on the site are the multi-column ones that tend to have parsing issues on Workday and other enterprise ATS.

Resume.io: ~30 templates, more variety in visual style. Similar reliance on multi-column layouts in the promotional templates.

Both: Do offer single-column, ATS-friendly options, but you often have to hunt for them because the visually striking templates dominate the featured selection.

ATS-friendliness

Neither tool includes a built-in ATS score checker. Both claim their templates are ATS-friendly, and single-column ones generally are, but multi-column designs on both platforms tend to parse poorly on Workday (the most widely used enterprise ATS).

If ATS-readiness matters to you, you'll want to run your finished resume through a separate ATS checker regardless of which tool you built it in. The free ATS resume checker from Applio works on any resume, regardless of where it was built.

AI features

Both Zety and Resume.io added AI-assisted writing in 2023-2024. Both use AI to suggest bullet-point phrasing and generate professional summaries. The quality is roughly comparable to what you'd get from GPT-4 with a well-written prompt.

Neither tool grounds AI writing in your real experience the way purpose-built AI resume tools do, the suggestions tend to be generic starting points you'll need to edit heavily.

Which one should you pick?

Pick Zety if: You want the more corporate/traditional template aesthetic and don't mind the ~$24/month or ~$71/year price point. Better for finance, consulting, and traditional business roles.

Pick Resume.io if: You want more template variety and a slightly cleaner UI. The 7-day trial is shorter than Zety's 14-day, so read the auto-renew terms carefully.

Pick something else if: You want a genuinely free download without a subscription trap, or you want a built-in ATS checker, or you're paying attention to which templates are actually ATS-friendly.

Frequently asked questions

Is Zety actually free?

No. Zety lets you build a resume for free but charges to download it. The trial is $2.95 for 14 days, then auto-renews to about $24/month unless cancelled.

Is Resume.io actually free?

No, in the same way as Zety. Build-free, pay-to-download. Trial $2.95 for 7 days, then ~$25/month.

Which is more ATS-friendly, Zety or Resume.io?

Both offer some ATS-friendly single-column templates but heavily promote multi-column designs that tend to parse poorly. Neither includes a built-in ATS checker.

Are Zety and Resume.io the same company?

They share a parent company (BOLD Ltd.). Feature parity and business model are close to identical.

How do I cancel my Zety or Resume.io subscription?

Both require going through account settings and clicking through several confirmation screens. Some users report needing to contact support directly to fully cancel. Check the terms of service and cancellation policy of your specific country before signing up.
