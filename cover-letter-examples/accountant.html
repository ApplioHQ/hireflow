Accountant cover letter example

Dear Hiring Manager,

I'm writing to apply for the Accountant position at [Company]. As a CPA with four years of experience in full-cycle accounting and month-end close, I bring the accuracy and discipline your finance team depends on.

In my current role I manage the general ledger, reconciliations, and financial reporting for a $30M business. I reduced our month-end close from 10 days to 5 by streamlining reconciliation workflows, and I identified $85K in billing discrepancies through systematic AR audits. I'm proficient in [ERP/software from posting], GAAP-compliant, and audit-ready in everything I touch.

I'd welcome the opportunity to bring that rigor to [Company]. Your focus on [specific detail] tells me this is a team that values getting the numbers right the first time.

Sincerely,
[Your Name]

How to write a accountant cover letter

  • Lead with your CPA or relevant credential
  • Quantify close time, cost savings, or accuracy improvements
  • Name specific software and GAAP compliance
  • Signal precision, discretion, and audit-readiness

Cover letter structure

Every strong cover letter follows the same three-part structure:

  • Opening — State the role, hook with your strongest, most relevant achievement, and show genuine interest in the company.
  • Body — Prove your fit with one or two quantified accomplishments that map to the job's requirements.
  • Closing — Connect to something specific about the company, restate your value, and invite the next step.

Frequently asked questions

What should an accountant emphasize in a cover letter?

Credentials (CPA), accuracy metrics, close time reductions, cost savings, and specific software. Accounting is a precision role, so let the numbers show it.

Do I need a CPA to apply?

Not for all roles. If you're CPA-eligible or working toward it, say so. Emphasize experience, accuracy, and relevant software.

How formal should the tone be?

Professional and precise. Accounting employers value clarity and reliability over flair.
