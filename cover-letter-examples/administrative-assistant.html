Administrative Assistant Cover Letter Example

Updated 2026-09-22 · Applio
An administrative assistant cover letter should show you keep everything running. Lead with the scope you managed and the time or money you saved.

Administrative Assistant cover letter example

Dear Hiring Manager,

I'm applying for the Administrative Assistant position at [Company]. I'm the person who keeps an office running smoothly, and I'd love to bring that reliability to your team.

In my current role I support a team of 20, managing calendars, travel, expense reports, and vendor relationships. I reorganized our scheduling and filing systems, cutting the time to book complex travel by half and eliminating double-bookings entirely. I'm proficient in Microsoft Office and [tools from posting], discreet with sensitive information, and calm when five things need doing at once.

I'd welcome the chance to support [Company]. Your posting's emphasis on [specific detail] tells me you need someone organized, proactive, and trustworthy, which is exactly how colleagues describe me.

Sincerely,
[Your Name]

How to write a administrative assistant cover letter

  • Lead with the size of the team or executives you supported
  • Quantify time or cost savings from process improvements
  • Name specific software and organizational systems
  • Convey discretion and composure

Cover letter structure

Every strong cover letter follows the same three-part structure:

  • Opening — State the role, hook with your strongest, most relevant achievement, and show genuine interest in the company.
  • Body — Prove your fit with one or two quantified accomplishments that map to the job's requirements.
  • Closing — Connect to something specific about the company, restate your value, and invite the next step.

Frequently asked questions

What makes a good admin assistant cover letter?

Evidence of organization at scale, process improvements with time/cost savings, software proficiency, and discretion. Show you make everyone around you more effective.

How do I show soft skills?

Through specifics, "reorganized filing to cut retrieval time in half" shows organization better than the word "organized."

Should I mention which executives I supported?

Yes, especially for C-suite or senior support. It signals the level of discretion and complexity you can handle.
