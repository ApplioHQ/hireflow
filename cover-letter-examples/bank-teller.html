Bank Teller cover letter example

Dear Hiring Manager,

I'm applying for the Bank Teller position at [Bank]. I combine precision with genuine customer care, and I'd be glad to represent your branch.

In my current role I process 150+ transactions a day with 99.9% accuracy, and I've balanced my drawer to the penny for 14 straight months. Beyond transactions, I've exceeded cross-sell referral targets by 130%, connecting customers to products that genuinely help them. I understand compliance, spot potential fraud, and treat every customer's money with the care I'd want for my own.

I'd welcome the chance to bring that accuracy and warmth to [Bank].

Sincerely,
[Your Name]

How to write a bank teller cover letter

  • Lead with transaction accuracy and drawer balancing
  • Show cross-sell or referral results
  • Mention compliance awareness and fraud detection
  • Balance precision with customer care

Cover letter structure

Every strong cover letter follows the same three-part structure:

  • Opening — State the role, hook with your strongest, most relevant achievement, and show genuine interest in the company.
  • Body — Prove your fit with one or two quantified accomplishments that map to the job's requirements.
  • Closing — Connect to something specific about the company, restate your value, and invite the next step.

Frequently asked questions

What should a bank teller cover letter emphasize?

Transaction accuracy, drawer-balancing record, cross-sell referrals, compliance awareness, and trustworthy, friendly customer service.

How do I get a teller job with no banking experience?

Emphasize any cash-handling and customer-service role. Highlight accuracy, math skills, and trustworthiness, which banks value most.

Should I mention sales or referrals?

Yes. Banks want tellers who can spot opportunities to refer customers to relevant products, so include any cross-sell results.
