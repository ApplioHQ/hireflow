Content Writer Cover Letter Example

Updated 2026-09-22 · Applio

Content Writer cover letter example

Dear Hiring Manager,

I'm writing to apply for the Content Writer position at [Company], and I hope this letter itself is a small writing sample. I create content that ranks, engages, and converts, and I'd love to bring that to your brand. My portfolio is at [portfolio link].

In my current role I write SEO articles, landing pages, and email campaigns. My content grew organic traffic by 70% year over year, and one pillar article I wrote ranks #1 for a competitive keyword and drives hundreds of signups a month. I research deeply, write clearly, and edit ruthlessly. I understand that good content serves the reader first and the algorithm second.

What draws me to [Company] is [specific content or audience detail]. I'd bring strong craft, SEO fluency, and writing that earns its place in someone's day.

Sincerely,
[Your Name]

How to write a content writer cover letter

  • Let the cover letter itself demonstrate your writing
  • Lead with traffic or conversion results
  • Show SEO knowledge and range (articles, pages, email)
  • Link writing samples or a portfolio

Cover letter structure

Every strong cover letter follows the same three-part structure:

  • Opening — State the role, hook with your strongest, most relevant achievement, and show genuine interest in the company.
  • Body — Prove your fit with one or two quantified accomplishments that map to the job's requirements.
  • Closing — Connect to something specific about the company, restate your value, and invite the next step.

Frequently asked questions

Should a content writer cover letter be a writing sample?

Yes, in effect. Make it clear, engaging, and error-free, because it demonstrates the exact skill you're being hired for.

How do I quantify writing work?

Organic traffic growth, keyword rankings, conversions or signups driven, and engagement metrics turn writing into business results.

Do I need SEO experience?

For most content roles, yes, at least a working knowledge. Mention keyword research, on-page optimization, and any measurable ranking wins.
