Dental Assistant cover letter example

Dear Hiring Manager,

I'm applying for the Dental Assistant position at [Practice]. I combine strong clinical skills with a calm, reassuring chairside manner, and I'd love to join your team.

In my current role I assist across restorative and hygiene procedures, take digital X-rays, manage sterilization, and prep operatories for a three-provider practice seeing 60+ patients a day. I'm certified in [certifications], proficient in [practice software], and known for keeping anxious patients at ease. I take pride in a smoothly run operatory where the dentist can focus entirely on care.

I'd welcome the chance to bring that clinical skill and warmth to [Practice].

Sincerely,
[Your Name]

How to write a dental assistant cover letter

  • Lead with certifications and clinical skills
  • Show the patient volume and pace you handle
  • Name procedures, X-ray, and sterilization experience
  • Emphasize chairside manner with anxious patients

Cover letter structure

Every strong cover letter follows the same three-part structure:

  • Opening: State the role, hook with your strongest, most relevant achievement, and show genuine interest in the company.
  • Body: Prove your fit with one or two quantified accomplishments that map to the job's requirements.
  • Closing: Connect to something specific about the company, restate your value, and invite the next step.

Frequently asked questions

What should a dental assistant cover letter include?

Certifications, clinical skills (X-rays, sterilization, chairside assisting), patient volume, practice software, and a reassuring chairside manner.

How do I stand out as a new dental assistant?

Lead with your certification and externship hours. Emphasize sterilization protocols, X-ray certification, and comfort with patients.

Should I mention specific software?

Yes. Dentrix, Eaglesoft, or Open Dental experience lets you start with minimal training.
