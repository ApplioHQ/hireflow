Executive Assistant Cover Letter Example

Updated 2026-09-22 · Applio
An executive assistant cover letter should signal you can support senior leaders with discretion and precision. Lead with the level you supported and the complexity you handled.

Executive Assistant cover letter example

Dear Hiring Manager,

I'm writing to apply for the Executive Assistant position at [Company]. I make executives more effective by owning the details, and I'd be glad to bring that to your leadership team.

In my current role I support the CEO and two VPs, managing complex calendars across time zones, booking international travel, and coordinating board materials. I anticipate needs before they're voiced, protect executive time ruthlessly, and handle confidential information with complete discretion. I streamlined our board-prep process, cutting turnaround from a week to two days, and I'm the calm center when priorities shift hourly.

I'd welcome the opportunity to support [Company]'s leadership. Your posting's emphasis on [specific detail] describes exactly the kind of high-trust, high-complexity role I excel in.

Sincerely,
[Your Name]

How to write a executive assistant cover letter

  • State the seniority of executives you supported
  • Show complexity: multi-timezone calendars, travel, board prep
  • Emphasize discretion and anticipating needs
  • Quantify a process improvement

Cover letter structure

Every strong cover letter follows the same three-part structure:

  • Opening — State the role, hook with your strongest, most relevant achievement, and show genuine interest in the company.
  • Body — Prove your fit with one or two quantified accomplishments that map to the job's requirements.
  • Closing — Connect to something specific about the company, restate your value, and invite the next step.

Frequently asked questions

What should an executive assistant cover letter show?

The seniority of leaders you supported, the complexity you managed (calendars, travel, board work), discretion, and proactive anticipation of needs.

How is an EA cover letter different from an admin one?

It emphasizes higher-stakes support, confidentiality, executive-level judgment, and the ability to operate with autonomy.

Should I name the executives I supported?

Reference their level (CEO, VP) rather than names. It signals the trust and complexity you're used to.
