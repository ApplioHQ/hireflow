Financial Analyst cover letter example

Dear Hiring Manager,

I'm writing to apply for the Financial Analyst role at [Company]. I build the models and analyses that leaders use to make decisions, and I'm eager to bring that rigor to your finance team.

In my current role I own financial modeling, forecasting, and variance analysis for a $50M business unit. My revised forecasting model cut variance from 12% to 4%, and a pricing analysis I built informed a decision that added $1.5M in annual margin. I'm advanced in Excel and [tools from posting], and I translate complex numbers into clear recommendations executives can act on.

What draws me to [Company] is [specific detail]. I'd bring analytical precision, business curiosity, and models that hold up under scrutiny.

Sincerely,
[Your Name]

How to write a financial analyst cover letter

  • Lead with a model or analysis that drove a decision
  • Quantify forecast accuracy, margin, or cost impact
  • Show Excel and financial software proficiency
  • Emphasize translating numbers into recommendations

Cover letter structure

Every strong cover letter follows the same three-part structure:

  • Opening — State the role, hook with your strongest, most relevant achievement, and show genuine interest in the company.
  • Body — Prove your fit with one or two quantified accomplishments that map to the job's requirements.
  • Closing — Connect to something specific about the company, restate your value, and invite the next step.

Frequently asked questions

What should a financial analyst cover letter include?

A specific model or analysis with measurable business impact, forecast accuracy or margin figures, and your ability to communicate to executives.

How technical should it be?

Show your modeling skill through results, not jargon. The goal is proving your analysis changed a decision.

Do I need certifications like the CFA?

Helpful but not required for most roles. If you're a candidate or charterholder, mention it. Otherwise emphasize modeling and impact.
