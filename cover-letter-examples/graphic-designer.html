Graphic Designer Cover Letter Example

Updated 2026-09-22 · Applio
A graphic designer cover letter should point to your portfolio and show business impact. Lead with a design that moved a metric, then link your work.

Graphic Designer cover letter example

Dear Hiring Manager,

I'm excited to apply for the Graphic Designer role at [Company]. I design work that's both beautiful and effective, and your brand's visual direction is one I'd love to help evolve. My portfolio is at [portfolio link].

In my current role I design across brand, digital, and campaign work. A rebrand and landing-page redesign I led lifted conversion by 30%, and I built a design system that cut our team's production time by a third. I'm fluent in the Adobe Creative Suite and Figma, and I collaborate closely with marketing and product to make sure design serves the goal, not just the aesthetic.

What draws me to [Company] is [specific brand or product detail]. I'd bring strong craft, systems thinking, and designs that earn their keep.

Sincerely,
[Your Name]

How to write a graphic designer cover letter

  • Link your portfolio prominently in the first paragraph
  • Show a design that moved a business metric
  • Name your tools: Adobe Creative Suite, Figma
  • Balance craft with business impact

Cover letter structure

Every strong cover letter follows the same three-part structure:

  • Opening — State the role, hook with your strongest, most relevant achievement, and show genuine interest in the company.
  • Body — Prove your fit with one or two quantified accomplishments that map to the job's requirements.
  • Closing — Connect to something specific about the company, restate your value, and invite the next step.

Frequently asked questions

Do designers need a cover letter if they have a portfolio?

The portfolio proves craft; the cover letter shows business impact, collaboration, and fit. Together they're stronger than either alone. Always link the portfolio.

How do I quantify design work?

Conversion lifts, production-time savings from design systems, engagement improvements, or campaign performance tie your craft to outcomes.

Which tools should I mention?

The ones in the posting, typically Adobe Creative Suite and Figma. Add any motion, 3D, or prototyping tools relevant to the role.
