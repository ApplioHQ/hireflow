Human Resources Manager Cover Letter Example

Updated 2026-09-22 · Applio
An HR manager cover letter should show you balance people and business. Lead with a program you built that improved retention, hiring, or culture.

Human Resources Manager cover letter example

Dear Hiring Manager,

I'm applying for the Human Resources Manager position at [Company]. I build HR programs that make companies better places to work while advancing business goals, and your commitment to [specific value] resonates with how I practice HR.

In my current role I lead HR for a 200-person organization, owning recruiting, employee relations, and compliance. I redesigned our onboarding, which cut new-hire time-to-productivity by 25%, and I brought voluntary turnover down 30% through a manager-training and engagement program. I handle sensitive situations with fairness and discretion, and I keep us compliant without slowing the business down.

I'd welcome the chance to bring that people-first, business-aware approach to [Company].

Sincerely,
[Your Name]

How to write a human resources manager cover letter

  • Lead with a program that improved retention or hiring
  • Quantify turnover reduction, time-to-hire, or engagement
  • Show you balance people needs with business goals
  • Convey fairness, discretion, and compliance knowledge

Cover letter structure

Every strong cover letter follows the same three-part structure:

  • Opening — State the role, hook with your strongest, most relevant achievement, and show genuine interest in the company.
  • Body — Prove your fit with one or two quantified accomplishments that map to the job's requirements.
  • Closing — Connect to something specific about the company, restate your value, and invite the next step.

Frequently asked questions

What should an HR manager cover letter show?

Programs you built with measurable impact (retention, time-to-hire, engagement), plus discretion, fairness, and compliance knowledge.

How do I show both empathy and business sense?

Pair a people-focused program with a business metric, "an engagement program that cut turnover 30%" shows both at once.

Should I mention HR certifications?

Yes. SHRM-CP/SCP or PHR/SPHR signal professionalism. Name them near the top.
