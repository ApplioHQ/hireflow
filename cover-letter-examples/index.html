Frequently asked questions

How do I use a cover letter example?

Study the structure and phrasing, then adapt it with your own role, company, and quantified achievements. Never copy it word for word, use it as a starting point and replace every placeholder.

Should every job application include a cover letter?

When a cover letter is optional, submitting a strong tailored one still helps you stand out. When it's required, a generic one hurts you, always tailor it to the specific role and company.

How long should a cover letter be?

Three to four short paragraphs on a single page, around 250-400 words. Recruiters skim, so lead with your strongest, most relevant achievement.
