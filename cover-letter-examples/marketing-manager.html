Marketing Manager Cover Letter Example

Updated 2026-09-22 · Applio
A marketing manager cover letter should lead with growth. Open with a campaign result, tie your strategy to the company's goals, and show you own metrics.

Marketing Manager cover letter example

Dear Hiring Manager,

I'm writing to apply for the Marketing Manager position at [Company]. I've spent the last six years building demand-generation programs that move real numbers, and your brand's trajectory in [market] is one I'd be thrilled to accelerate.

At my current company I own a multi-channel program spanning paid, content, email, and lifecycle marketing. Last year my campaigns drove a 45% increase in qualified leads and a 3.2x return on ad spend while cutting cost-per-lead by 20%. I'm equally comfortable in the data and the creative brief, and I've managed a team of four while partnering closely with sales and product.

What excites me about [Company] is [specific product, audience, or challenge]. I'd bring a growth mindset, rigorous measurement, and campaigns that are as creative as they are accountable.

Sincerely,
[Your Name]

How to write a marketing manager cover letter

  • Open with a campaign result (leads, ROAS, growth %)
  • Show you own metrics and attribution
  • Balance creative and analytical skills
  • Connect your strategy to the company's specific market

Cover letter structure

Every strong cover letter follows the same three-part structure:

  • Opening — State the role, hook with your strongest, most relevant achievement, and show genuine interest in the company.
  • Body — Prove your fit with one or two quantified accomplishments that map to the job's requirements.
  • Closing — Connect to something specific about the company, restate your value, and invite the next step.

Frequently asked questions

How do I quantify marketing results in a cover letter?

Use lead growth, ROAS, cost-per-lead, pipeline influenced, or revenue attributed. Specific percentages and multiples are far more persuasive than adjectives.

Should a marketing cover letter be creative?

It should show voice and clarity, but substance wins. Lead with results; let the writing quality demonstrate your craft.

What if I'm moving from specialist to manager?

Highlight cross-channel results, any team or project leadership, and your ability to connect marketing to revenue.
