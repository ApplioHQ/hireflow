Mechanical Engineer cover letter example

Dear Hiring Manager,

I'm applying for the Mechanical Engineer position at [Company]. I design mechanical systems that work reliably in the real world, and your work in [industry/product] is exactly the kind of engineering I want to do.

In my current role I design components and systems from concept through production. One assembly I redesigned reduced manufacturing cost by 18% while improving durability, and I cut a product's development cycle by two months through better DFM practices. I'm proficient in SolidWorks and [tools from posting], experienced with GD&T and tolerance analysis, and I collaborate closely with manufacturing to make sure designs are actually buildable.

I'd welcome the chance to bring that practical, results-oriented approach to [Company].

Sincerely,
[Your Name]

How to write a mechanical engineer cover letter

  • Lead with a product or system you designed and its impact
  • Quantify cost reduction, cycle time, or performance gains
  • Name CAD tools and technical methods (GD&T, FEA, DFM)
  • Show collaboration with manufacturing

Cover letter structure

Every strong cover letter follows the same three-part structure:

  • Opening — State the role, hook with your strongest, most relevant achievement, and show genuine interest in the company.
  • Body — Prove your fit with one or two quantified accomplishments that map to the job's requirements.
  • Closing — Connect to something specific about the company, restate your value, and invite the next step.

Frequently asked questions

What should a mechanical engineer cover letter include?

A design project with measurable results (cost, durability, cycle time), CAD and analysis tools, and evidence you design for manufacturability.

How do new grad engineers write one?

Lead with senior design projects, internships, and CAD skills. Quantify anything you can and show hands-on, buildable thinking.

Should I mention specific software?

Yes. SolidWorks, CATIA, ANSYS, and similar tools are keywords. Name the ones in the posting that you know.
