Recruiter Cover Letter Example

Updated 2026-09-22 · Applio

Recruiter cover letter example

Dear Hiring Manager,

I'm excited to apply for the Recruiter position at [Company]. I find and hire great people quickly, and I'd love to help you build your team in [function or market].

In my current role I own full-cycle recruiting across technical and business roles. Last year I made 45 hires with an average time-to-fill of 28 days, 30% faster than our team average, while maintaining a 90% offer-acceptance rate. I source creatively, screen rigorously, and give candidates an experience that makes them say yes. I partner closely with hiring managers to calibrate quickly and avoid wasted cycles.

What draws me to [Company] is [specific growth or hiring challenge]. I'd bring a strong sourcing engine, a great candidate experience, and a track record of hiring quality at speed.

Sincerely,
[Your Name]

How to write a recruiter cover letter

  • Lead with hires made, time-to-fill, and acceptance rate
  • Show sourcing creativity and candidate experience
  • Demonstrate hiring-manager partnership
  • Balance speed with quality-of-hire

Cover letter structure

Every strong cover letter follows the same three-part structure:

  • Opening — State the role, hook with your strongest, most relevant achievement, and show genuine interest in the company.
  • Body — Prove your fit with one or two quantified accomplishments that map to the job's requirements.
  • Closing — Connect to something specific about the company, restate your value, and invite the next step.

Frequently asked questions

What should a recruiter cover letter show?

Hires made, time-to-fill, offer-acceptance rate, sourcing approach, and how you partner with hiring managers to hire quality at speed.

How do agency and in-house recruiting differ on paper?

Agency emphasizes volume, placements, and client management; in-house emphasizes candidate experience and hiring-manager partnership. Tailor accordingly.

Should I mention my ATS experience?

Yes. Naming systems like Greenhouse, Lever, or Workday shows you can start productively right away.
