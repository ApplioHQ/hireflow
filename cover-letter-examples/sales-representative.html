Sales Representative cover letter example

Dear Hiring Manager,

I'm excited to apply for the Sales Representative role at [Company]. I've consistently exceeded quota for the past three years, and I'm eager to bring that track record to a product I believe in.

In my current role I carry a $1.2M annual quota and finished last year at 128% of target, ranking #2 of 24 reps. I built my pipeline through disciplined prospecting, consultative discovery, and relentless follow-up, and I closed the largest new-logo deal in my region. I don't just hit numbers; I build relationships that renew and expand.

What draws me to [Company] is [specific product or market]. I'd bring hunger, a proven sales process, and the kind of persistence that turns a cold list into closed revenue.

Sincerely,
[Your Name]

How to write a sales representative cover letter

  • Lead with quota attainment (%) and revenue
  • Include your ranking among peers
  • Show your sales process, not just results
  • Convey hunger and persistence

Cover letter structure

Every strong cover letter follows the same three-part structure:

  • Opening — State the role, hook with your strongest, most relevant achievement, and show genuine interest in the company.
  • Body — Prove your fit with one or two quantified accomplishments that map to the job's requirements.
  • Closing — Connect to something specific about the company, restate your value, and invite the next step.

Frequently asked questions

How do I quantify sales results?

Quota attainment percentage, revenue closed, ranking among reps, deal size, and pipeline generated. Numbers are the currency of a sales cover letter.

What if I missed quota last year?

Lead with your best period or a specific big win, and frame growth. Show process and resilience, which sales managers respect.

Should a sales cover letter be aggressive?

Confident and specific, not arrogant. Let your numbers do the selling and show you understand consultative, relationship-driven sales.
