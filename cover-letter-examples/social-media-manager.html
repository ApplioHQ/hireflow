Social Media Manager Cover Letter Example

Updated 2026-09-22 · Applio
A social media manager cover letter should show you grow audiences and drive results. Lead with follower or engagement growth and tie it to business outcomes.

Social Media Manager cover letter example

Dear Hiring Manager,

I'm excited to apply for the Social Media Manager role at [Company]. I build engaged audiences that turn into real business results, and your brand's voice is one I'd love to amplify.

In my current role I own social strategy across Instagram, TikTok, and LinkedIn. I grew our combined following from 40K to 180K in 18 months and drove a 60% increase in social-sourced website traffic. I plan content calendars, create and edit, analyze performance, and jump on trends fast. I know the difference between vanity metrics and the ones that actually move the business.

What draws me to [Company] is [specific brand or audience detail]. I'd bring creativity, data fluency, and a feel for what makes people stop scrolling.

Sincerely,
[Your Name]

How to write a social media manager cover letter

  • Lead with follower and engagement growth
  • Tie social metrics to traffic or revenue
  • Name the platforms and content types you own
  • Show you distinguish vanity metrics from real ones

Cover letter structure

Every strong cover letter follows the same three-part structure:

  • Opening — State the role, hook with your strongest, most relevant achievement, and show genuine interest in the company.
  • Body — Prove your fit with one or two quantified accomplishments that map to the job's requirements.
  • Closing — Connect to something specific about the company, restate your value, and invite the next step.

Frequently asked questions

What should a social media manager cover letter show?

Audience and engagement growth, business results (traffic, leads, revenue), platform expertise, and content creation skills.

Should I link my own social profiles?

If they show relevant work or growth you drove, yes. A strong portfolio of managed accounts is powerful proof.

How do I show ROI on social?

Connect growth to outcomes, social-sourced traffic, leads, conversions, or sales, rather than followers alone.
