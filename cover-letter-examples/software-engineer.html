Software Engineer Cover Letter Example

Updated 2026-09-22 · Applio
A software engineer cover letter should show impact, not just list technologies. Lead with a shipped project and a measurable result, then connect your skills to what the team is building.

Software Engineer cover letter example

Dear Hiring Manager,

I'm excited to apply for the Software Engineer role at [Company]. Over the past four years I've built and shipped production systems in a fast-moving environment, and the problems your team is solving in [product area] are exactly the kind of work I want to do next.

In my current role I led the rewrite of our payments service, cutting p99 latency by 40% and reducing failed transactions by a third. I work primarily in [languages/frameworks from the posting], and I care as much about clean, tested, maintainable code as I do about shipping fast. I've mentored two junior engineers, both of whom now own major features.

What draws me to [Company] is [specific detail about the company or product]. I'd bring a bias toward measurable impact, strong collaboration across product and design, and a track record of shipping reliable software at scale.

Sincerely,
[Your Name]

How to write a software engineer cover letter

  • Lead with a shipped project and a number, not a list of languages
  • Mirror the exact technologies named in the job posting
  • Show collaboration and mentorship, not just individual coding
  • Reference something specific about the company or product

Cover letter structure

Every strong cover letter follows the same three-part structure:

  • Opening — State the role, hook with your strongest, most relevant achievement, and show genuine interest in the company.
  • Body — Prove your fit with one or two quantified accomplishments that map to the job's requirements.
  • Closing — Connect to something specific about the company, restate your value, and invite the next step.

Frequently asked questions

How long should a software engineer cover letter be?

Three to four short paragraphs on a single page. Recruiters skim, so lead with your strongest, most quantified achievement.

Should I list programming languages in my cover letter?

Mention the ones named in the job posting in context, but don't just list them. The resume carries the full skills list; the cover letter shows impact.

Do engineers even need cover letters?

Many roles are won on the resume and portfolio, but a sharp, specific cover letter still differentiates you, especially at companies that read them.
