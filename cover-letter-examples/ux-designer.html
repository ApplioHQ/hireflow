UX Designer Cover Letter Example

Updated 2026-09-22 · Applio
A UX designer cover letter should show research-driven design that improved outcomes. Lead with a redesign and its measurable impact, and link your portfolio.

UX Designer cover letter example

Dear Hiring Manager,

I'm applying for the UX Designer position at [Company]. I design experiences grounded in research that make products easier and more delightful to use. My portfolio is at [portfolio link].

In my current role I own end-to-end UX from research through high-fidelity design. A checkout redesign I led, informed by usability testing, reduced cart abandonment by 25%. I run user interviews, build wireframes and prototypes in Figma, and partner tightly with engineering and product to ship. I design with data and empathy in equal measure, and I always test my assumptions with real users.

What excites me about [Company] is [specific product or design challenge]. I'd bring rigorous research, strong craft, and a track record of designs that measurably improve the experience.

Sincerely,
[Your Name]

How to write a ux designer cover letter

  • Link your portfolio in the first paragraph
  • Lead with a redesign and its metric impact
  • Show your research process, not just visuals
  • Name your tools: Figma, prototyping, usability testing

Cover letter structure

Every strong cover letter follows the same three-part structure:

  • Opening — State the role, hook with your strongest, most relevant achievement, and show genuine interest in the company.
  • Body — Prove your fit with one or two quantified accomplishments that map to the job's requirements.
  • Closing — Connect to something specific about the company, restate your value, and invite the next step.

Frequently asked questions

What should a UX designer cover letter include?

A research-driven project with measurable impact, your process (research, wireframing, testing), tools like Figma, and a portfolio link.

How important is the portfolio?

Essential. The cover letter shows impact and process; the portfolio proves craft. Link it prominently.

Should I emphasize research or visual design?

Match the posting. Product-focused roles value research and testing; brand-adjacent roles weigh visual craft more heavily.
