Warehouse Worker Cover Letter Example

Updated 2026-09-22 · Applio
A warehouse worker cover letter should show reliability, safety, and productivity. Lead with your throughput and safety record.

Warehouse Worker cover letter example

Dear Hiring Manager,

I'm applying for the Warehouse Worker position at [Company]. I'm a reliable, safety-focused worker who keeps the operation moving, and I'd be glad to join your team.

In my current role I pick, pack, and ship 200+ orders per shift while maintaining a 99.5% accuracy rate. I'm forklift-certified with a perfect safety record over three years, and I know my way around RF scanners and warehouse management systems. I show up on time, hit my numbers, and look out for my teammates' safety.

I'd welcome the chance to bring that dependability to [Company]. Your posting's emphasis on [specific detail] is exactly the kind of team I want to be part of.

Sincerely,
[Your Name]

How to write a warehouse worker cover letter

  • Lead with throughput (orders/units per shift) and accuracy
  • Highlight forklift certification and safety record
  • Name equipment: RF scanners, WMS, pallet jacks
  • Emphasize reliability and attendance

Cover letter structure

Every strong cover letter follows the same three-part structure:

  • Opening — State the role, hook with your strongest, most relevant achievement, and show genuine interest in the company.
  • Body — Prove your fit with one or two quantified accomplishments that map to the job's requirements.
  • Closing — Connect to something specific about the company, restate your value, and invite the next step.

Frequently asked questions

What should a warehouse cover letter highlight?

Throughput, accuracy rate, forklift certification, safety record, equipment experience, and dependable attendance. Employers value reliability and safety above all.

Do I need a cover letter for warehouse jobs?

It's not always required, but a short one showing your safety record and reliability helps you stand out from applicants who skip it.

Should I mention certifications?

Yes. Forklift certification, OSHA training, and equipment experience are strong differentiators.
