How to Beat the Ashby ATS (2026 Guide)

Updated 2026-09-22 · Applio
Ashby is a modern, analytics-driven Applicant Tracking System used by many high-growth startups. It emphasizes recruiter workflows, structured hiring, and data over automated resume rejection. Your resume should clearly show quantified, relevant experience and match the job's key skills so it parses correctly and makes a fast, strong impression on the hiring team.

How the Ashby ATS works

Ashby parses your resume into a candidate profile and supports keyword search, structured scorecards, and detailed hiring analytics. Recruiters and hiring managers review applications directly and evaluate candidates on structured criteria. Automated rejection is minimal, the focus is on efficient human review and structured decision-making.

What Ashby looks for

  • Quantified, relevant achievements a recruiter can scan quickly
  • Keywords matching the role for search
  • Experience mapped to the role's core competencies
  • A clean, parseable profile

How to format your resume for Ashby

  • Single-column layout with standard headings
  • Lead with impact and quantify results
  • Mirror the specific skills and tools in the posting
  • Keep it concise and scannable
  • Text-based PDF export

Common Ashby mistakes

  • Vague, unquantified bullets, Ashby-using startups hire on demonstrated impact
  • Multi-column or graphic layouts that break parsing
  • Missing the exact skills the role screens for

Frequently asked questions

Does Ashby auto-reject resumes?

Ashby does minimal automated rejection. It's built for structured, efficient human review. Your resume needs to make a fast, strong, quantified impression and match the role's key skills.

What kind of companies use Ashby?

Ashby is popular with fast-growing, data-driven startups and scale-ups. These companies tend to hire on demonstrated, quantified impact, so lead with measurable achievements.

Do keywords matter in Ashby?

Yes, recruiters search by keyword, so include the role's exact skills and tools. But quantified impact and clear relevance carry the most weight.
