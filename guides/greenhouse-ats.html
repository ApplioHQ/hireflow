How to Beat the Greenhouse ATS (2026 Guide)

Updated 2026-09-22 · Applio
Greenhouse is a popular Applicant Tracking System among tech companies and startups. Unlike keyword-heavy enterprise systems, Greenhouse relies less on automated resume rejection and more on recruiter review plus structured interview scorecards. Your resume needs to clearly show relevant, quantified experience that a recruiter can scan in seconds, and match the job's key requirements so you surface in keyword searches.

How the Greenhouse ATS works

Greenhouse parses your resume into a candidate profile and stores the original file. Recruiters and hiring managers review applications directly and rate candidates on structured scorecards tied to the role's attributes. Greenhouse supports keyword search across applications, and many companies add screening questions, but there is less automated filtering than in enterprise ATS platforms.

What Greenhouse looks for

  • Clear, quantified achievements a recruiter can scan quickly
  • Keywords matching the job's required skills for recruiter search
  • Relevant experience mapped to the role's scorecard attributes
  • A clean, parseable structure so your profile populates correctly

How to format your resume for Greenhouse

  • Lead each role with your strongest quantified bullet, recruiters scan top-down
  • Use a single-column layout with standard headings for clean parsing
  • Mirror the skills and tools named in the job description
  • Keep it to one page for early-career, two for senior roles
  • Export as a text-based PDF

Common Greenhouse mistakes

  • Writing vague duty statements instead of measurable results, Greenhouse companies interview on impact
  • Burying relevant experience below unrelated roles
  • Ignoring the job description's exact skill names, which recruiters search for

Frequently asked questions

Does Greenhouse automatically reject resumes?

Greenhouse does less automated rejection than enterprise systems. Most filtering happens through recruiter review and optional screening questions. Your resume's job is to make a fast, strong impression on a human and to surface in keyword searches.

What is a Greenhouse scorecard?

A scorecard is a structured evaluation form interviewers fill out, rating you on specific attributes tied to the role. Aligning your resume bullets to the role's core competencies helps interviewers score you higher.

Do keywords matter in Greenhouse?

Yes, recruiters search applications by keyword, so include the exact skills and tools from the job posting. But quantified impact matters more here than raw keyword density.
