How to Beat the Lever ATS (2026 Guide)

Updated 2026-10-10 · Applio
Lever is a modern Applicant Tracking System and candidate CRM used by many high-growth companies. It emphasizes recruiter sourcing and relationship management over automated screening. Your resume is parsed into a profile that recruiters search and tag, so clear structure, exact skill keywords, and quantified achievements are what get you surfaced and shortlisted.

Who uses the Lever ATS

Lever is a modern applicant tracking system common at startups and growth-stage technology companies. It doubles as a recruiting CRM, so Lever teams actively source, tag, and nurture candidates over time rather than only reacting to inbound applications. If you are applying to a venture-backed or fast-growing company, there is a good chance it runs on Lever, and understanding its relationship-driven model helps you stand out.

How the Lever ATS works

Lever parses your resume into a searchable candidate profile and keeps the original attached. Recruiters search and filter their talent pool by keywords, tags, and stages, and they often source passive candidates. There is minimal automated rejection, applications flow to recruiters who review and advance candidates through pipeline stages.

What Lever looks for

  • Exact skill and tool keywords recruiters search for
  • Quantified, scannable achievements
  • A clean profile parse with correct titles and dates
  • Relevant experience that matches the role's must-haves

How to format your resume for Lever

  • Single-column layout with standard section headings
  • Put your most relevant skills and tools in a dedicated Skills section
  • Use consistent, recognizable date formats
  • Lead with impact, quantify wherever possible
  • Submit a text-based PDF

Common Lever mistakes

  • Relying on graphics or columns that break the profile parse
  • Omitting the specific tools and technologies named in the posting
  • Writing responsibilities instead of results

How Lever handles your application

Lever does not auto-reject resumes on keywords. It parses your resume into a profile, makes it searchable, and lets recruiters tag and track candidates across roles. Because Lever is relationship and sourcing focused, referrals and a clear, keyword-aligned resume both help. A resume that parses cleanly and matches the exact skills in the posting is easy for a recruiter to find, shortlist, and move into their pipeline.

Step by step: applying through Lever

  1. Submit a single-column, text-based resume so the parser builds your profile correctly.
  2. Fill in any additional fields or questions, and add your LinkedIn or portfolio when offered.
  3. Match the required skills and tools from the job description in your bullets, using the posting's exact terms.
  4. If you know someone at the company, ask for a referral, since Lever teams lean on sourcing and relationships.
  5. Keep your most relevant, recent experience at the top where a recruiter scanning the pipeline will see it first.

Frequently asked questions

Does Lever auto-reject applications?

Lever does little automated rejection. Applications go to recruiters who review them and move candidates through pipeline stages. Your resume needs to be clear, keyword-relevant, and quantified so a recruiter advances you.

How do recruiters find candidates in Lever?

They search the candidate database by keyword, tags, and pipeline stage, and they source passive candidates. Including exact skill keywords from the job posting improves your chances of surfacing.

What file format works best with Lever?

A text-based PDF parses cleanly. Avoid scanned images, tables, and multi-column layouts that scramble the parsed profile.

Does Lever reject resumes automatically?

No. Lever parses your resume into a searchable profile for recruiters and does not auto-reject on keywords. Clean formatting and a strong keyword match to the role are what get you surfaced.

Does a referral help in Lever?

Yes. Lever is built around sourcing and candidate relationships, so referrals are often tracked directly in the system and can get your application prioritized.

What should my Lever resume focus on?

Clean parsing plus an obvious match to the job post. Lead with recent, relevant experience and mirror the exact required skills so recruiters find and shortlist you quickly.
