How to Beat the Workable ATS (2026 Guide)

Updated 2026-09-22 · Applio
Workable is an Applicant Tracking System popular with small and mid-size companies. It parses your resume into a profile, offers AI-assisted candidate suggestions, and lets hiring teams review and rate applicants collaboratively. A clean, keyword-relevant, quantified resume that parses correctly is what surfaces you and impresses the reviewers.

How the Workable ATS works

Workable parses your resume into a candidate profile and can suggest how well you match the role using AI. Hiring teams review applications, leave comments, and move candidates through pipeline stages. There is light automated ranking but heavy reliance on collaborative human review.

What Workable looks for

  • Keyword and skills relevance to the posting
  • Quantified, scannable achievements
  • Clean parsing of your work history
  • Clear fit with the role's core requirements

How to format your resume for Workable

  • Single-column layout with standard section headings
  • Include a Skills section mirroring the job's requirements
  • Lead roles with quantified impact
  • Use consistent, recognizable date formats
  • Submit a text-based PDF

Common Workable mistakes

  • Columns, tables, or graphics that scramble the parse
  • Duty-based bullets with no measurable results
  • Missing the specific skills the hiring team is filtering for

Frequently asked questions

Does Workable automatically rank candidates?

Workable offers AI-assisted matching suggestions, but hiring happens through collaborative human review. A clean, keyword-relevant, quantified resume both scores well and reads well to the team.

What formatting works best in Workable?

A single-column resume with standard headings and a text-based PDF export parses reliably. Avoid tables, columns, and graphics.

Do I need exact keywords for Workable?

Include the job's key skills and tools for relevance and search, but Workable's human-centric review also rewards clear, quantified impact.
