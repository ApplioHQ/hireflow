How to Beat the Workday ATS (2026 Guide)

Updated 2026-09-22 · Applio
Workday is an enterprise Applicant Tracking System used by most Fortune 500 companies. It parses your uploaded resume into a structured profile, then asks you to verify or re-enter that data manually. Workday ranks candidates on keyword match against the job requisition and screening questions, so a clean single-column resume with exact keyword matches and correctly parsed work history is what gets you through.

How the Workday ATS works

When you apply through Workday, you upload a resume and the system auto-fills a profile from it. Workday's parser is strict about structure: it reads standard section headings and reverse-chronological dates. It then presents the parsed data for you to confirm. Recruiters filter the candidate pool by keywords, years of experience, and answers to screening questions.

What Workday looks for

  • Exact keyword matches from the job requisition, especially required skills and certifications
  • Correctly parsed job titles, companies, and employment dates in reverse-chronological order
  • Standard section headings ("Work Experience," "Education," "Skills")
  • Complete answers to knockout screening questions (years of experience, work authorization, location)

How to format your resume for Workday

  • Use a single-column layout, Workday mangles two-column resumes and puts skills in the wrong fields
  • Write dates as "Jan 2022 – Present" or "01/2022," Workday needs a recognizable date format to build your work history
  • Never put contact info in the header/footer, Workday's parser often skips it
  • Upload a text-based PDF or .docx, not a scanned image
  • Match the exact job title from the posting somewhere in your resume if it fits your experience

Common Workday mistakes

  • Trusting the auto-parsed profile without checking it, Workday frequently misassigns dates and titles
  • Using tables or text boxes, which scramble in Workday's parser
  • Leaving screening questions blank or answering a knockout question wrong (these auto-reject you)

Frequently asked questions

Does Workday auto-reject resumes?

Workday itself does not auto-reject on resume content, but knockout screening questions (work authorization, minimum experience) can automatically disqualify you. The resume affects your keyword ranking, which determines whether a recruiter ever opens your application.

Why does Workday scramble my resume when I upload it?

Workday's parser struggles with multi-column layouts, tables, and text boxes. Use a single-column, standard-heading resume and always review the auto-filled profile before submitting.

Should I fill out the Workday profile or just upload my resume?

Do both, and correct any parsing errors in the profile. Recruiters search the structured profile data, so mistakes there hurt your ranking even if your resume is perfect.
