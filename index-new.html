Is Applio really 100% free?

Yes, completely. Every feature is free and unlimited: the resume builder, all 26 templates, unlimited PDF downloads with no watermark, the ATS checker, AI tailoring, cover letters, interview prep, and job tracker. No paid plans, and no credit card required.

What's the catch?

How does the AI tailor my resume?

You paste a job description, and Applio rewrites your bullets, matches the keywords, and sharpens your summary to fit the role, grounded only in your real experience. It never invents facts.

What is an ATS and why does it matter?

An Applicant Tracking System is software most employers use to scan and filter resumes before a person reads them. If yours lacks the right keywords or uses formatting the system can't parse, it gets filtered out. Applio scores your resume against the ATS and tells you exactly what to fix.

Will my resume work with recruiters' systems?

Yes. Every template is a clean, single-column, ATS-safe layout, and the built-in checker scores your resume against any job so you know it'll parse before you apply.
