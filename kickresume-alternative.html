Updated August 2026 · Applio

Why choose Applio

  • Free where it counts. Build and export a real resume for free (no credit card), and check your ATS score against any job with no sign-up.
  • ATS-first. Applio scores your resume against a specific posting and lists the exact missing keywords and fixes.
  • More than a resume. AI tailoring, cover letters, interview prep, and career tools (Skill Gap, Brag Doc, First 90 Days, Salary Insights) in one place.
  • Honest AI. Applio's AI is grounded in your real experience, it never invents job titles, employers, numbers, or skills.
  • No download paywall surprises. Watermark-free export on every plan, with a one-time lifetime option if you want all AI features forever.

What Kickresume does well

Kickresume has strong AI writing powered by GPT, nice modern templates, and a useful personal website builder. If you want AI-generated content and a portfolio site alongside your resume, it is worth considering. Applio and Kickresume both aim to help you land interviews, this page is to help you pick the right fit for how you work.

Is Applio a good free Kickresume alternative?

How does Applio AI compare to Kickresume AI?

Both use AI for resume writing. Applio focuses on tailoring your resume to a specific job posting with ATS optimization, while Kickresume uses GPT for general content generation. Applio also adds AI interview prep and career tools.

Does Applio have a personal website builder like Kickresume?

Applio focuses on resume building, ATS scoring, interview prep, and career tools rather than personal websites. If a portfolio site is your priority, Kickresume offers that; if ATS optimization and job-specific tailoring matter more, Applio is the stronger choice.
