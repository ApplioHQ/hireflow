Why convert LinkedIn to a resume at all?

Sending someone your LinkedIn PDF looks like it should be enough. It isn't. Here's why:

LinkedIn PDF as-is

  • Multi-column layout confuses ATS parsers
  • LinkedIn icons render as garbled characters in some ATS
  • Wrong length for most jobs (usually 3-6 pages instead of 1-2)
  • Can't tailor to a specific job description
  • Signals "I didn't put in effort" to recruiters

Converted resume in Applio

  • Single-column layout, ATS-friendly by default
  • Real selectable text on every element
  • Trimmed to 1-2 pages, focused on relevance
  • One-click tailor to any job description
  • Recruiters see a real resume, not a LinkedIn dump

How Applio's LinkedIn PDF parsing works

Applio's PDF parser reads LinkedIn's profile export format directly. Instead of dumping the raw PDF into your resume as one giant text block, it recognizes LinkedIn's structural conventions:

  • About section → maps to your professional summary
  • Experience block → each role becomes an editable experience entry with company, title, dates, and bullets
  • Education → each degree becomes an education entry with school, degree, dates, honors
  • Skills → your listed skills become your skills section
  • Licenses & Certifications → maps to certifications
  • Volunteer, Projects, Publications → their own sections

You can edit any field before exporting, add sections LinkedIn doesn't include (like a targeted summary), and switch between 25 templates without losing content.

What to fix before you export

LinkedIn's writing style is different from resume style. After the import, spend 5 minutes on these common cleanups:

  • Rewrite your summary. LinkedIn's "About" section is often first-person and biographical. A resume summary should be third-person and lead with your title + years + one accomplishment.
  • Cut generic responsibilities. LinkedIn tends to list job duties. Strong resumes lead with specific accomplishments with numbers.
  • Trim older roles. A 15-year career on LinkedIn is fine; on a resume, focus on the last 10-15 years and summarize older work.
  • Add missing skills that match the job. Use the free ATS checker to see which keywords are missing for a specific role.
  • Quantify at least 50% of your bullets. Applio's AI Improve can help — it prompts you for a number whenever a bullet is vague.
