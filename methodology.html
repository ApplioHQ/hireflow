How Applio creates career guidance

Updated November 20, 2026 · Applio editorial policy

Applio is designed to help people make clearer job-search decisions. This page explains what our tools evaluate, where their limits are, and how we handle factual claims in our educational content.

Resume and ATS guidance

Our guidance favors readable, text-based resumes with clear section headings, accurate job titles, relevant skills, and specific accomplishments. An ATS check compares the resume and job description for practical signals such as keyword overlap, standard sections, readability, and missing information.

It is not a simulation of a specific employer's hiring system. Every employer configures its recruiting process differently, so an ATS score is a diagnostic aid, not a prediction of an interview or offer.

AI suggestions

AI features use the resume and job description you provide to propose wording, structure, and prioritization. They are designed to preserve the facts in your input. You should review every suggestion before using it and remove anything that is incomplete, inaccurate, or not yours.

Applio does not promise a hiring outcome. A strong resume can improve clarity and relevance, but experience, timing, employer criteria, and many other factors affect a hiring decision.

Salary insights

Salary outputs are estimates intended to support research and negotiation preparation. They can vary with location, level, skills, industry, company size, bonuses, equity, and the job market. Treat a result as a starting point and compare it with current, role-specific public data before deciding what to ask for.

Editorial and source policy

For factual career-market claims, we prefer primary sources and transparent methodology. These include government labor data, official employer or ATS documentation, and studies that disclose their sample or method. We avoid presenting viral resume statistics as facts when their original source or methodology cannot be verified.

Educational pages are updated when guidance, source material, or product behavior changes. If you spot a factual error or an outdated source, email support@appliohq.com.
