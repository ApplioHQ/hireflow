Novoresume Alternative: Applio

Updated August 2026 · Applio
Applio is a free alternative to Novoresume with stronger AI and ATS features. Novoresume offers attractive templates and a guided editor popular in Europe, but its free plan adds a watermark to your resume. Applio gives you watermark-free exports on every plan, a free ATS checker, and AI that tailors your resume to a specific job posting.

Why choose Applio

  • Free where it counts. Build and export a real resume for free (no credit card), and check your ATS score against any job with no sign-up.
  • ATS-first. Applio scores your resume against a specific posting and lists the exact missing keywords and fixes.
  • More than a resume. AI tailoring, cover letters, interview prep, and career tools (Skill Gap, Brag Doc, First 90 Days, Salary Insights) in one place.
  • Honest AI. Applio's AI is grounded in your real experience, it never invents job titles, employers, numbers, or skills.
  • No download paywall surprises. Watermark-free export on every plan, with a one-time lifetime option if you want all AI features forever.

What Novoresume does well

Novoresume has well-designed, modern templates and a guided editor that walks you through each section. It is especially popular in Europe and is a good starting point if you value a visual, template-first approach. Applio and Novoresume both aim to help you land interviews, this page is to help you pick the right fit for how you work.

Frequently asked questions

Is Applio a good free Novoresume alternative?

Yes. Applio lets you build and export a watermark-free resume for free, and adds a free ATS checker and AI tailoring that Novoresume does not offer on its free plan.

Does Novoresume have AI like Applio?

Novoresume offers content suggestions to help you fill in sections, but it does not have full AI tailoring to a specific job posting, AI cover letter generation, or AI interview prep like Applio.

Does Applio export without a watermark?

Yes. Applio exports are watermark-free on every plan, including the free tier. Novoresume's free plan adds a watermark to your resume, and removing it requires a paid subscription.
