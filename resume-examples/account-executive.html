Frequently asked questions

What should an account executive resume show?

Quota attainment, ARR/revenue closed, ranking, win rate, and pipeline self-sourcing.

Enterprise vs SMB?

Tailor to the role. Enterprise emphasizes deal size and multi-stakeholder sales; SMB emphasizes velocity and volume.

How do I stand out?

Lead with your strongest number and show a repeatable sales process.
