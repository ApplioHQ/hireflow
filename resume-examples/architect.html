Frequently asked questions

Should I include my portfolio link?

Yes. Architecture is visual — include a link to your online portfolio or personal website. Make sure it loads fast and showcases your best 5-8 projects.

What software should architects list?

Revit, AutoCAD, SketchUp, Rhino, and rendering tools (Enscape, V-Ray, Lumion). BIM proficiency is increasingly expected. List your specific version experience.

How do I show project scope?

Construction value, square footage, project type, and team size managed. Also mention code jurisdictions, sustainability certifications, and client types.
