Frequently asked questions

What should an auditor resume include?

Audit scope (client size, controls tested), findings and their impact, and compliance frameworks.

CPA or CIA?

CPA (external) or CIA (internal) strengthen your profile. List if you have or are pursuing them.

Internal vs external audit?

Tailor to the role, external emphasizes financial statements; internal emphasizes controls and risk.
