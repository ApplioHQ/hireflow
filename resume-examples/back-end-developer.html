Frequently asked questions

What should a back-end resume highlight?

Scalability metrics (requests, uptime, latency), your language and database stack, and system-design experience.

Do I need cloud experience?

Increasingly yes. Name AWS, GCP, or Azure services you actually use.

How do I show system design?

Describe an architecture decision and its measurable outcome, e.g. a migration or a caching layer.
