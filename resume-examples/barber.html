Frequently asked questions

What should a barber put on their resume?

State license, years of experience, client book size, specialties (fades, razor cuts, coloring), and any continuing education or competition wins.

How do I quantify barbering experience?

Cuts per day, rebooking rate, revenue generated, client retention, and social media following all demonstrate your value.

Should I include my social media presence?

Absolutely. A strong Instagram or TikTok with before/after photos is essentially a portfolio for barbers. Include the handle and follower count.
