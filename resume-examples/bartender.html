Frequently asked questions

Should I include TIPS or ServSafe certification?

Yes. Many employers require responsible beverage service certification. List it prominently, it can be a filter in ATS systems for hospitality roles.

How do I quantify bartending experience?

Use revenue generated per shift, guests served, check size increases, waste reduction, or new hires trained. Numbers make a bartending resume stand out.

What if I'm applying to a different industry?

Highlight transferable skills: customer service, cash handling, fast-paced multitasking, upselling (sales), inventory management. These translate across industries.
