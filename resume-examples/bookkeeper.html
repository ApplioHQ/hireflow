Frequently asked questions

What software should a bookkeeper list?

QuickBooks (Online and Desktop), Xero, FreshBooks, and Excel are essential. Add any payroll platforms (Gusto, ADP) and industry-specific tools.

Do bookkeepers need certifications?

Not required, but a Certified Bookkeeper (CB) from AIPB or QuickBooks ProAdvisor certification demonstrates competence and professionalism.

How do I show accuracy on a bookkeeper resume?

Mention error rates, audit results, reconciliation accuracy, and on-time reporting. Bookkeeping is a precision role — numbers prove it.
