Frequently asked questions

What should a carpenter resume show?

The types of work you do (framing, finish, remodel), inspection record, and safety.

Do tradespeople need resumes?

A short one showing your skills, safety record, and reliability helps you stand out.

Should I list certifications?

Yes. OSHA training, apprenticeship completion, and any specialty certs help.
