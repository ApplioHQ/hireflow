Frequently asked questions

Should a chef resume include culinary school?

Yes, but your work experience matters more. List culinary education but lead with professional kitchen experience and concrete results.

How do I quantify chef experience?

Use covers per night, food cost percentage, team size, health scores, average ticket time, and revenue impact. Numbers separate professionals from hobbyists.

Should I list every restaurant I worked at?

Focus on the last 3-5 positions that show progression. Too many short stints can raise concerns about job-hopping.
