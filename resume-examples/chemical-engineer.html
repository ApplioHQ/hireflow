Frequently asked questions

What should a chemical engineer resume show?

Process improvements with yield/cost/safety metrics, simulation tools, and scale-up or GMP experience.

Should I list Aspen or simulation tools?

Yes. Aspen Plus/HYSYS and process-safety methods (HAZOP, PHA) are keywords.

How do I show safety focus?

Include safety reviews you led and any incident-free records or audit results.
