Civil Engineer Resume Examples & Template

Updated 2026-08-29 · Applio
A civil engineer resume should feature your PE or EIT status, specific project types (transportation, structural, water resources), software (AutoCAD, Civil 3D, Revit), and project scale (budget, area, structures designed).

Frequently asked questions

Should I list my PE or EIT on my resume?

Absolutely. Put it right after your name (e.g., "Jane Doe, PE"). PE licensure is often a hard requirement and the most important credential.

How do I describe projects on a civil engineering resume?

Name the project type, your specific role, the scope (budget, area, structures), and the outcome. Use numbers: $XM budget, X miles of roadway, X structures designed.

Which software should I highlight?

AutoCAD Civil 3D is near-universal. Also list Revit, MicroStation, HEC-RAS, ArcGIS, or any specialized software the posting mentions.
