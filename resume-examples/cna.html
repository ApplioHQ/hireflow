Frequently asked questions

What should a CNA put on their resume?

State certification, BLS/CPR, patient-to-staff ratios you handled, care settings (hospital, SNF, home health), and any specialty experience (dementia, pediatrics).

How do I stand out as a CNA applicant?

Quantify your patient load, highlight perfect attendance, mention specialized training (dementia care, wound care), and include any awards or recognitions.

Should CNAs include clinical hours from training?

Yes, especially if you are newly certified. List the facility, hours completed, and skills practiced.
