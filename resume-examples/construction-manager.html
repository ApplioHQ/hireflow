Frequently asked questions

What certifications help a construction manager resume?

PMP, CCM (Certified Construction Manager), OSHA 30, and LEED AP are the most recognized. List them prominently near your name.

How should I present project experience?

Lead each role with the largest or most complex project. Include project type, dollar value, square footage, and timeline performance.

Should I list software skills?

Yes. Procore, PlanGrid, Bluebeam, Primavera P6, and MS Project are commonly expected. Name the ones you actually use.
