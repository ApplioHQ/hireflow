Frequently asked questions

Should a copywriter use a resume or a portfolio?

Both. The resume shows your career arc and measurable results; the portfolio shows your writing. Link to the portfolio prominently.

How do I quantify copywriting results?

Conversion rates, click-through rates, revenue generated, open rates, ROAS, and A/B test wins with percentage lifts.

What skills should a copywriter highlight?

Match the job: direct response roles want conversion metrics; brand roles want tone-of-voice and campaign experience. Always include SEO and analytics.
