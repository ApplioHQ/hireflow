Frequently asked questions

Which cybersecurity certifications should I list?

Lead with CompTIA Security+ (entry-level), CISSP (experienced), or CEH. List them in your header or a dedicated certifications section, not buried in skills.

How do I show impact in cybersecurity?

Quantify: incidents responded to, MTTR improvements, vulnerabilities remediated, false positive reduction rates, compliance audits passed, or endpoints monitored.

Should I include a home lab or CTF experience?

Yes, especially early career. A home lab (SIEM setup, malware analysis) or CTF placements show hands-on skills beyond certifications.
