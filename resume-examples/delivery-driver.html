Frequently asked questions

What should a delivery driver put on their resume?

License class, delivery volume, on-time rate, safety record (miles without incident), and customer satisfaction scores.

Do I need a CDL for delivery driver jobs?

Not for most last-mile delivery roles (Amazon, UPS, FedEx). A clean Class C license works. CDL is needed for larger commercial vehicles.

How do I stand out as a delivery driver?

Safety record and customer ratings are king. Zero accidents, high on-time percentages, and top rankings among peers make a strong case.
