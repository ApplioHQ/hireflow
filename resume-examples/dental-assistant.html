Frequently asked questions

Do dental assistants need certification?

Requirements vary by state. CDA (DANB) certification is nationally recognized and preferred by most employers. Radiology certification is required in most states.

What makes a dental assistant resume strong?

Specific procedure experience, patient volume, software proficiency, and X-ray certification. Quantify your daily patient load and procedure types.

Should I list expanded functions?

Yes. If your state allows expanded duties (impressions, temporaries, coronal polishing), list them — they significantly increase your value to employers.
