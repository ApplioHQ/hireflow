Frequently asked questions

What software should a dental receptionist list?

Dentrix, Eaglesoft, Open Dental, and any imaging software. Also list general tools like Microsoft Office and any patient communication platforms.

How do I quantify dental front office experience?

Patient volume per day, collection rates, no-show rates, claim denial reductions, and case acceptance improvements all demonstrate your impact.

Do I need dental experience to apply?

Not always. Medical office, insurance, or customer service experience transfers well. Highlight scheduling, insurance, and patient interaction skills.
