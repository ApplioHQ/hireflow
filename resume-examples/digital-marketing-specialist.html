What should a digital marketing resume show?

Channel results (ROAS, traffic, conversion), budget managed, and analytics fluency.

Should I list certifications?

Yes. Google Ads, Google Analytics, and HubSpot certifications help.

Generalist or specialist?

Show range but lead with the channel where you have the strongest results.
