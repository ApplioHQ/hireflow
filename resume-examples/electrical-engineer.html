Frequently asked questions

What should an electrical engineer resume include?

Design projects with measurable outcomes (efficiency, cost, reliability), your tools, and validation experience.

Should I list specific tools?

Yes. Altium, SPICE, MATLAB, and oscilloscope/lab equipment are keywords.

How do new grads stand out?

Lead with senior design projects, internships, and hands-on lab and CAD skills.
