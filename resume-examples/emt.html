Frequently asked questions

What certifications should an EMT list?

NREMT-B or NREMT-P, state license, BLS/ACLS/PALS, PHTLS, ITLS, and any hazmat or technical rescue certifications.

How do I describe EMT experience without violating HIPAA?

Use aggregate metrics: call volume, response times, protocol compliance rates, and save rates. Never include patient-identifying details.

Should I include volunteer EMS experience?

Absolutely. Many EMTs start as volunteers. List it the same as paid experience with call volumes and responsibilities.
