Entry Level Resume Examples & Template

Updated 2026-09-04 · Applio
An entry-level resume proves you can do the job even without years of directly relevant experience. Lead with your education and any internships, then fill out with projects, part-time work, and campus or volunteer leadership. Quantify every bullet so recruiters see impact, not just tasks. Keep it to one page.

Entry level resume summary example

Recent finance graduate from [University] with two summer internships at [Bank] and [Firm], most recently building a DCF model for a $40M acquisition analysis presented to the deal team. Comfortable with Excel, Bloomberg, and financial modeling. Seeking an entry-level financial analyst role where I can contribute quickly and continue learning in a rigorous environment.

Illustrative example. Replace with your degree, university, and actual internships or projects.

What to include on an entry-level resume

  1. Contact info: Name, email, phone, LinkedIn, portfolio or GitHub if relevant
  2. Professional summary: 2-3 sentences on who you are and what role you want
  3. Education: University, degree, graduation date, GPA (if 3.5+), honors, relevant coursework
  4. Experience: Internships, part-time jobs, volunteer roles, research
  5. Projects: Class capstones, personal builds, hackathons, competitions
  6. Skills: Technical tools, languages, certifications
  7. Activities and awards: Leadership roles, scholarships, competitions

Entry level resume bullet point examples

Internship experience:

  • Built a portfolio performance dashboard used by 6 analysts during my summer internship, cutting weekly reporting time from 4 hours to 30 minutes.
  • Supported a 3-person consulting team on a client project, delivering a market sizing analysis that informed the final $2M investment recommendation.
  • Wrote 14 SEO-optimized blog posts during my marketing internship, generating 12,000+ organic visits in three months.

Projects and capstone work:

  • Led a 4-person team through a semester-long product management capstone, delivering a user-tested prototype presented to a panel of 5 industry judges.
  • Built an inventory tracking web app (React, Firebase) as a personal project, used by a family-owned business to manage 400+ SKUs.
  • Analyzed 3 years of Airbnb listing data in Python for a data analytics course project, identifying 4 pricing patterns that predicted occupancy with 82% accuracy.

Part-time work and leadership:

  • Trained 8 new baristas at a busy campus coffee shop, contributing to the highest customer satisfaction scores in the store's 5-year history.
  • Elected treasurer of the Investment Club, managing a $10K portfolio that outperformed the S&P 500 by 4.2% over the academic year.

Every bullet includes a specific action and a measurable outcome. Do the same with yours.

Common entry-level resume mistakes

  • Writing "no experience": Never say this. Frame what you've done, projects, courses, part-time work, volunteering, as the experience you have.
  • Using an objective statement: Objectives ("seeking a role where I can grow") are outdated. Use a professional summary instead.
  • Listing generic soft skills: "Team player," "hard worker," "detail-oriented" carry no weight. Show these through your bullets.
  • Including irrelevant early jobs: If you're applying to a marketing role, that summer as a lifeguard doesn't need three bullets. Keep it to one line if you include it at all.
  • Sending the same resume everywhere: Tailor each application. Even small tweaks to your summary and top bullets meaningfully improve response rates.

Frequently asked questions

What is an entry-level resume?

An entry-level resume is designed for candidates seeking their first full-time role after school or after a career break. It emphasizes education, internships, projects, and transferable skills.

How do I write an entry-level resume with no experience?

Lead with education and any internships. Fill out the rest with projects, part-time work, volunteer experience, and campus leadership. Every entry should show what you did and any measurable result.

How long should an entry-level resume be?

One page. Recruiters expect a single page. If you're struggling to fill it, expand your projects section or add relevant coursework rather than stretching bullets.

Should I include an objective on an entry-level resume?

Skip the objective and use a professional summary instead. An objective tells the recruiter what you want; a summary tells them what you bring, which is more persuasive.
