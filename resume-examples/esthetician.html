Frequently asked questions

What should an esthetician resume show?

License, clientele, rebooking rate, treatments offered, revenue, and retail sales.

Should I list specific treatments?

Yes. Facials, peels, microdermabrasion, and waxing show your service range.

How do I stand out?

Client retention, ratings, revenue, and any advanced certifications.
