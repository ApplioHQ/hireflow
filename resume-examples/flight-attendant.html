Frequently asked questions

What qualifications do flight attendants need?

FAA certification (earned through airline training), CPR/AED certification, a valid passport, and the ability to swim. Most airlines require a high school diploma; many prefer college education.

Should I include language skills?

Absolutely. Multilingual flight attendants are in high demand, especially for international routes. List languages and proficiency level (conversational, fluent, native).

How do I stand out as a flight attendant applicant?

Customer service experience, language skills, cultural adaptability, and a professional appearance. Quantify your service experience — passengers served, commendations received, flight hours logged.
