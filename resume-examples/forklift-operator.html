Frequently asked questions

What certifications should a forklift operator list?

OSHA forklift certification is required. List the specific equipment types you're certified on: sit-down counterbalance, reach truck, order picker, pallet jack.

How do I make a forklift operator resume stand out?

Safety record is number one. Zero accidents over X years/hours is powerful. Add pallet volume, accuracy rates, and any training you've done.

Should I include warehouse experience beyond forklift?

Yes. Shipping, receiving, inventory counts, and WMS experience make you more versatile and hireable.
