Frequently asked questions

Should I list both frontend and backend skills?

Yes. Full stack roles require breadth. Organize skills into categories (frontend, backend, database, DevOps) to show depth in each area.

How do I show I am truly full stack?

Describe projects where you owned the entire stack — database schema, API design, frontend UI, and deployment. End-to-end ownership is the key signal.

Is full stack developer the same as software engineer?

Overlapping but distinct. Full stack emphasizes breadth across the web stack. Software engineer is broader and may include systems, mobile, or infrastructure. Tailor your title to match the job posting.
