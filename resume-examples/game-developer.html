Frequently asked questions

What should a game developer resume show?

Shipped titles, your engine (Unity/Unreal), the systems you owned, and performance optimizations.

Should I link a demo?

Absolutely. A playable build, itch.io page, or gameplay reel is powerful proof.

C++ or C#?

Depends on the engine. Unreal favors C++; Unity favors C#. Match the studio.
