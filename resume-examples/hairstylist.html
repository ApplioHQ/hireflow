Frequently asked questions

What should a hairstylist resume show?

License, clientele size, rebooking rate, revenue, specialties, and social presence.

Should I include social media?

Yes. A strong Instagram of your work is essentially a portfolio for stylists.

How do I quantify styling work?

Client book size, rebooking rate, service and retail revenue.
