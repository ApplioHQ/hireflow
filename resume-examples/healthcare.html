Healthcare & Public Service Resume Examples

Updated 2026-07-09 · Applio
Healthcare and public-service resumes are screened for credentials first: lead with your active license and certifications, then back them with patient, client, or student outcomes and the volumes you handled. Keep the layout clean and parseable so nothing gets lost.

Registered Nurse Resume Examples

Social Worker Resume Examples
A strong social worker resume leads with your licensure and the populations you serve, then shows measurable impact on client outcomes and caseload management.
Teacher Resume Examples
A strong teacher resume highlights your state certification, grade level and subject expertise, and classroom outcomes like improved test scores or engagement.
