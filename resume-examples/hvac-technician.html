Frequently asked questions

What certifications do HVAC technicians need?

EPA 608 certification is required for handling refrigerants. NATE certification is preferred by most employers. List both prominently with certification numbers.

How do I quantify HVAC experience?

Systems serviced per year, first-visit fix rate, energy savings achieved for customers, and safety record. Specialties (VRF, geothermal, commercial chillers) add value.

Should I list specific equipment brands?

Yes. Listing manufacturer-specific training (Carrier, Trane, Lennox, Daikin) shows you can work on the systems employers and customers have installed.
