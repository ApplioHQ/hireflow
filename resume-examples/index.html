How to use these resume examples

A resume example is a starting point, not a template to copy. Here's how to get the most from them:

  1. Study the structure. Notice which sections are included, how the summary is phrased, and where skills are placed. That's the blueprint for your role.
  2. Adapt the bullets. Replace the example's specifics with your own numbers, projects, and outcomes. Keep the format: action verb + what you did + measurable result.
  3. Match keywords. Compare the example's skills section to the job description you're targeting. Add the keywords the posting uses — that's what ATS filters scan for.
  4. Score before you send. Run your finished resume through the ATS checker to catch missing keywords and formatting issues.

Frequently asked questions

How do I use a resume example?

Pick the example for your role, study the summary, bullet points, and skills sections, then adapt them with your own experience and numbers. Don't copy word-for-word — use the structure and phrasing as a starting point.

Are these examples ATS-friendly?

Yes. Every example uses standard section headings, quantified bullet points, and role-specific keywords that Applicant Tracking Systems scan for.

Should I use the same resume for every job?

No. Tailoring your resume to each job description significantly increases response rates. Use the example as your base, then adjust keywords and bullet emphasis for each application. Applio's AI tailoring tool does this in seconds.
