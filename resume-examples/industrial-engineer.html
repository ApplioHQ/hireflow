Frequently asked questions

What certifications help an industrial engineer?

Six Sigma (Green/Black Belt) and Lean certifications are strong differentiators.

How do I quantify process work?

Throughput, defect rate, cost savings, cycle time, and inventory reductions.

What tools should I list?

Simulation software, AutoCAD, statistical tools (Minitab), and ERP systems.
