Frequently asked questions

What licenses do insurance agents need?

Property & Casualty (P&C) and Life & Health licenses are the foundation. List your specific state licenses and any designations (CPCU, CIC, CLU).

How do I show sales success?

Annual premium written, retention rate, policies per household, bind rate, and referral percentage. Revenue and retention are the two metrics that matter most.

Should I specialize or be a generalist?

Specializing (commercial lines, high-net-worth, or benefits) typically commands higher compensation. Show your specialty but mention breadth to demonstrate versatility.
