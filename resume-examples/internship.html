What to include on an internship resume

  1. Contact info: Name, email, phone, LinkedIn, portfolio or GitHub if relevant
  2. Education: University, degree, expected graduation, GPA (if 3.5+), relevant coursework
  3. Projects: Class projects, personal builds, hackathons, competitions — often more valuable than jobs for first internships
  4. Experience: Any part-time work, tutoring, volunteer roles, campus jobs, research
  5. Skills: Technical tools, languages, software, certifications
  6. Activities: Clubs, leadership positions, athletics, competitions

Internship resume bullet point examples

Projects:

  • Built a full-stack expense tracker (React, Node.js, MongoDB) as a personal project, used by 6 friends to split shared costs across 4 months.
  • Led a 4-person marketing capstone for a local restaurant, increasing weekend reservations by 32% over 6 weeks through targeted Instagram ads.
  • Placed 2nd of 38 teams at [University] Case Competition 2025 for a market entry strategy proposal analyzed by 3 industry judges.

Part-time or volunteer work:

  • Tutored 12+ students in introductory statistics, contributing to an average grade improvement of one full letter across the semester.
  • Managed the social media accounts for a student-run coffee shop, growing Instagram followers from 240 to 1,800 in one semester.
  • Volunteered as a research assistant, cleaning and analyzing survey data from 400+ respondents in R for a faculty paper.

Campus leadership:

  • Elected treasurer of the Business Society, managing a $12K annual budget across 8 events and delivering the first year-end surplus in 4 years.
  • Organized a career panel of 6 alumni that drew 120+ attendees, the largest turnout for a club event that semester.

Every bullet includes a specific action and a measurable outcome. Do the same with yours.

How to tailor your internship resume

  • Read the internship posting closely: Note the required and preferred skills, and the language they use
  • Reorder projects to lead with relevance: A finance internship should see your finance project first, not your gaming side project
  • Mirror the posting's keywords: If they mention "financial modeling" and you built one, use those exact words
  • Adjust your summary: Two sentences that match the specific internship, not a generic student pitch

Frequently asked questions

What should a first-time internship resume include?

Contact info, education, relevant coursework, projects, part-time or volunteer work, technical skills, and extracurricular leadership. Class projects and campus activities give you plenty of substance even without previous internships.

How do I write an internship resume with no experience?

Lead with education, then a strong projects section. Class projects, hackathons, personal builds, and volunteer work all count. Frame each with what you did, the tools you used, and the outcome.

Should I include my high school?

Only if you're a freshman in your first semester of college. By sophomore year, drop it and focus on college work and projects.

How long should an internship resume be?

One page. Recruiters expect a single page for students and interns. Cut anything unrelated to the internship.
