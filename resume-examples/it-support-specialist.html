Frequently asked questions

What certifications help an IT support resume?

CompTIA A+, Network+, and Security+ are the baseline. Microsoft certifications (AZ-900, MS-900) and ITIL Foundation add value.

How do I quantify IT support work?

Tickets resolved per day, first-contact resolution rate, satisfaction scores, users supported, and systems deployed are all strong metrics.

Should I list all the software I support?

List the most relevant ones: OS platforms, productivity suites, ticketing systems, and any enterprise applications specific to the job posting.
