Frequently asked questions

How do I make a custodian resume stand out?

Use numbers: square footage maintained, inspection scores, supply cost savings, and response times for maintenance requests.

Should I list certifications?

Yes. OSHA 10, bloodborne pathogens, floor care certification, and any equipment licenses (floor buffers, carpet extractors) show professionalism.

Is custodial experience transferable?

Very. Facility management, maintenance, healthcare EVS, and property management all value the same core skills.
