Frequently asked questions

Do librarians need an MLS degree?

Most professional librarian positions require an MLS/MLIS from an ALA-accredited program. Library assistant and technician roles may not require graduate education.

How do I show impact as a librarian?

Circulation statistics, program attendance, reference volume, grant funding secured, and collection usage metrics. Librarians are increasingly measured on community impact.

Should I include technology skills?

Yes. ILS proficiency, database management, digital resource platforms, and basic coding/web skills are increasingly expected. Name the specific systems you know.
