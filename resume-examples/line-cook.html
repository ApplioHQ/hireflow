Frequently asked questions

What should a line cook resume show?

Stations worked, covers per shift, food-safety certification, and consistency under volume.

Do I need ServSafe?

It helps a lot and is required by many kitchens. List it if you have it.

How do I stand out?

Volume handled, consistency, speed, and any station leadership or training.
