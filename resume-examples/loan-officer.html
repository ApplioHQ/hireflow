Frequently asked questions

What should a loan officer put on their resume?

NMLS ID, production volume (dollar amount and units), pull-through rate, loan types (conventional, FHA, VA, jumbo), and LOS platforms.

How do I show production numbers?

Annual funded volume in dollars and units is the key metric. Include ranking against peers and percentage of target achieved.

Should I list compliance training?

Yes. SAFE Act, TRID, RESPA/TILA, and fair lending training show you understand the regulatory landscape. This matters to compliance-conscious employers.
