Frequently asked questions

What software should logistics coordinators know?

TMS (Transportation Management System), WMS (Warehouse Management System), SAP, Oracle, and advanced Excel. Name the specific platforms you have used.

How do I show logistics impact?

On-time delivery rate, cost savings from negotiations, shipments managed per month, and error/discrepancy resolution rates. Efficiency and reliability are the key metrics.

Do I need supply chain certifications?

APICS CSCP or CLTD, and Six Sigma certifications strengthen your resume. They are not required for coordinator roles but signal career investment.
