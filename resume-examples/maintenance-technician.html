Frequently asked questions

What certifications help a maintenance technician resume?

EPA 608 (refrigerant handling), OSHA 10/30, electrical journeyman license, and any manufacturer-specific certifications. CMRT (Certified Maintenance & Reliability Technician) is a differentiator.

How should I organize multi-trade experience?

List your strongest trades in the skills section. In bullet points, show versatility by including examples from electrical, plumbing, HVAC, and mechanical work.

Should I include the CMMS systems I use?

Yes. Name them — Maximo, SAP PM, eMaint, Fiix. Facilities value technicians who can document work properly.
