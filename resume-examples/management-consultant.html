Frequently asked questions

What should a consultant resume show?

Client impact in dollars, the problems you solved, analytical rigor, and stakeholder leadership.

How do I quantify consulting work?

Savings/revenue identified, efficiency gains, and the scale of engagements and teams.

Is an MBA required?

Helpful for some firms but not universal. Emphasize analytical and problem-solving results.
