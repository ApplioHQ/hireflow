Frequently asked questions

What credentials should a massage therapist list?

Your state license (LMT), national certification (NCBTMB), CPR/First Aid, and any modality-specific certifications like neuromuscular therapy.

How do I show business results on a massage therapy resume?

Include client retention rates, rebooking percentages, revenue generated, and client book size. These matter in spa and clinic settings.

Should I list all modalities I know?

List the ones relevant to the position. A sports clinic cares about sports massage and myofascial release; a spa cares about Swedish and hot stone.
