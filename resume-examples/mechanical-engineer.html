Frequently asked questions

Which CAD software should I list on my resume?

List the CAD tools you know and that appear in the job posting, most commonly SolidWorks, CATIA, Creo, or AutoCAD. Naming the right software is often a screening filter for engineering roles.

Should I include the FE or PE license?

Yes, if you hold the Fundamentals of Engineering (FE/EIT) or Professional Engineer (PE) license, list it, since it can be required or preferred for certain mechanical engineering positions.

How do I quantify mechanical engineering work?

Use metrics like cost reduction, weight savings, efficiency or performance gains, defect and failure reductions, production yield, and on-time milestone delivery.
