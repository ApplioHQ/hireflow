Frequently asked questions

Should I specialize in iOS or Android?

Specializing in one platform deeply is usually better than being mediocre at both. However, cross-platform experience (React Native, Flutter) is increasingly valued.

Do app download numbers matter?

Yes. Downloads, ratings, and active users prove real-world impact. Also mention retention, crash rates, and App Store ranking improvements.

Should I include personal apps?

Yes, especially if they have downloads and ratings. Side projects show initiative and let you demonstrate skills outside NDA-protected work.
