Frequently asked questions

What credentials do occupational therapists need?

OTR/L (Occupational Therapist, Registered/Licensed) after passing the NBCOT exam. List your state license number and any specialty certifications (CHT, SCLV, etc.).

How do I quantify OT outcomes?

Functional independence scores, caseload size, treatment plan success rates, and length of stay reductions. Patient outcomes are the primary measure of clinical effectiveness.

Should I list continuing education?

List relevant CEUs and specialty certifications. Focus on areas that match your target role — pediatric, hand therapy, neuro rehab, etc.
