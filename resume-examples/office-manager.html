Frequently asked questions

What should an office manager resume include?

Operational scope (headcount, budget), vendor and facilities management, and process improvements with savings.

How do I show impact?

Cost savings, budget managed, headcount supported, and efficiency gains.

Is HR experience relevant?

Yes. Onboarding, benefits coordination, and light HR support strengthen the profile.
