Frequently asked questions

What certification should I list?

NASM, ACE, NSCA-CPT, and ACSM are the most recognized. List your certification, ID, and expiration. Specialty certifications (CES, PES) add value.

How do I show impact as a trainer?

Client results (weight loss, strength gains, goal achievement rates), retention rates, revenue generated, and roster size. Trainers are evaluated on outcomes.

Should I include my own fitness background?

Briefly, if it is relevant (competitive athlete, transformation story). But lead with professional certifications and client results.
