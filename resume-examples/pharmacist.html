Frequently asked questions

What credentials should a pharmacist list on their resume?

Lead with your PharmD, state license with number, and any board certifications (BCPS, BCOP). Include DEA registration if applicable.

How do pharmacists quantify their impact?

Use prescription volume per day, error rates, immunizations administered, cost savings from formulary management, and patient counseling metrics.

Should I include retail and clinical experience separately?

Yes. Use distinct entries highlighting the different skill sets — dispensing speed for retail, clinical interventions for hospital settings.
