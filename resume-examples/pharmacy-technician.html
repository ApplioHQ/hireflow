Frequently asked questions

Should I list my CPhT certification on my resume?

Absolutely. Put it in your header or summary. CPhT certification is the single most important credential for pharmacy technician roles and most employers require or strongly prefer it.

How do I quantify pharmacy technician experience?

Use numbers: prescriptions processed per shift, error rates, inventory value managed, claims recovered, or technicians trained. Even estimates (X+ per shift) beat vague descriptions.

What pharmacy software should I mention?

Name the systems you know: PioneerRx, QS/1, McKesson, Rx30, ScriptPro, or Epic. ATS filters often match on exact software names.
