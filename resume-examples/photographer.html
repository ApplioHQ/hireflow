Frequently asked questions

Should a photographer use a traditional resume or a portfolio?

Both. A resume gets past ATS and HR screens; link to your portfolio for creative review. Include the portfolio URL prominently.

How do I quantify photography work?

Use sessions per year, client satisfaction rates, repeat booking percentages, turnaround times, and revenue generated.

What technical skills should I list?

Name your camera systems, editing software (Lightroom, Photoshop, Capture One), lighting equipment, and any specializations like drone or video.
