Frequently asked questions

Should I list my DPT and licensure on the resume?

Yes. Put "DPT" after your name and list your state licensure prominently. These are non-negotiable requirements for PT roles.

How do I quantify physical therapy outcomes?

Use patient outcome percentages (% meeting goals), caseload volume (patients/day), treatment duration improvements, or satisfaction scores.

What specializations should I highlight?

Name them: orthopedic, neurological, pediatric, geriatric, sports, or women's health. Board-certified specialties (OCS, NCS) should be listed after your name.
