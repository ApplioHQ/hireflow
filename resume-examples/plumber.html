Frequently asked questions

What license should I list?

List your journeyman or master plumber license, state, and license number. Specialty certifications (backflow, medical gas) add significant value.

How do I stand out as a plumber?

Customer ratings, first-visit fix rate, project volume, and safety record. Also mention specialty skills like tankless water heaters, radiant heating, or commercial fire suppression.

Should I include my apprenticeship?

Yes. List your apprenticeship program, hours completed, and the contractor where you trained. This demonstrates formal training and code knowledge.
