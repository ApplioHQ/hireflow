Frequently asked questions

What should a police officer resume include?

POST certification, academy training, specialized training (CIT, DUI, investigations), physical fitness scores, and community engagement activities. Clearance rate and complaint record demonstrate effectiveness.

How do I transition from law enforcement to private sector?

Emphasize transferable skills: investigation, report writing, conflict resolution, crisis management, leadership, and compliance. Translate police jargon into business language.

Should I include firearms qualifications?

For law enforcement roles, yes — include qualification scores and any instructor certifications. For private sector transitions, list it under certifications but do not lead with it.
