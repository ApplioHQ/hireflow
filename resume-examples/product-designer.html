What should a product designer resume show?

End-to-end projects with metric impact, your process, tools, and a portfolio link.

How important is the portfolio?

Essential. Link it prominently; product design is judged by seeing the work.

Research or visual?

Show both. Product roles value research-driven decisions and strong craft.
