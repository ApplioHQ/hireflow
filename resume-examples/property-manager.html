Frequently asked questions

What certifications help a property manager resume?

CPM (Certified Property Manager), ARM (Accredited Residential Manager), and CAM (Certified Apartment Manager) from IREM or NAA.

How do I show financial impact?

Include portfolio size (units, square footage), occupancy rates, NOI increases, rent collection rates, and capital project budgets managed.

Should I list property management software?

Yes. Yardi, AppFolio, RealPage, Buildium, and MRI are common. Name the ones you use — they are ATS keywords.
