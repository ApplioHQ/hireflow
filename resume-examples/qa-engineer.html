Frequently asked questions

Should I focus on manual or automation testing?

Both. Modern QA roles expect automation skills, but exploratory and manual testing remain valuable. Show progression toward automation while demonstrating testing judgment.

What automation frameworks should I know?

Selenium, Cypress, or Playwright for web; Appium for mobile; Postman or REST Assured for APIs. The specific framework matters less than demonstrating you can build and maintain test suites.

How do I show QA impact on a resume?

Defect detection rate, automation coverage, time saved, escaped defects prevented, and test execution speed. Quality is measured by what does NOT reach production.
