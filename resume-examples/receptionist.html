Frequently asked questions

What skills should a receptionist highlight?

Front desk management, phone systems, scheduling software, and customer service. Quantify where possible — calls handled per day, visitor volume, or accuracy rates.

Should I include typing speed on my resume?

Yes, if it is 50+ WPM. Many receptionist roles require fast, accurate data entry and typing speed is a concrete, verifiable skill.

Is a receptionist resume one page?

Yes. One page is standard for receptionist roles at all experience levels.
