Frequently asked questions

What ATS experience should I list?

Name the specific systems: Greenhouse, Lever, Workday, iCIMS, or Taleo. Recruiters are expected to be proficient in at least one major ATS.

How do I quantify recruiting success?

Time-to-fill, offer acceptance rate, hires per month, pipeline size, source diversity, and quality of hire (retention at 6/12 months). Show you fill roles fast with candidates who stay.

Should I specialize in technical or corporate recruiting?

Specializing in technical recruiting (engineering, product) commands higher compensation. List the specific function and level (IC vs. executive) you recruit for.
