Frequently asked questions

What credentials should an RT list?

RRT credential, state license, ACLS/PALS/NRP, and any specialty certifications.

How do I quantify RT work?

Patient load, care setting acuity, ventilator management, and outcome contributions.

Should I list care settings?

Yes. ICU, ER, NICU, and floor experience each signal different skill sets.
