Frequently asked questions

What metrics should a retail manager highlight?

Revenue, same-store sales growth, team size, turnover rate, shrinkage reduction, and customer satisfaction scores. Numbers prove leadership impact.

How long should a retail manager resume be?

One page for under 10 years of experience. Two pages are acceptable for senior retail leaders managing multiple locations.

Should I include hourly retail jobs from early career?

Only if they show progression (associate → shift lead → assistant manager → manager). Otherwise, focus on management roles.
