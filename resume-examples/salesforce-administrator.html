Frequently asked questions

What certifications matter for a Salesforce admin?

Salesforce Certified Administrator is the baseline; Advanced Administrator and Platform App Builder stand out.

Do I need to code?

Not required, but basic Apex and Flow skills expand what you can do and are increasingly expected.

How do I quantify admin work?

User counts, adoption rates, automation time savings, and data-quality metrics.
