Frequently asked questions

Which Scrum Master certification should I get?

CSM (Scrum Alliance) and PSM (Scrum.org) are the most recognized. PSM II or CSP-SM demonstrate advanced competency. List certification and ID number.

How do I show Scrum Master impact?

Velocity improvement, cycle time reduction, sprint commitment accuracy, and team satisfaction scores. Also mention impediments resolved and process improvements implemented.

Should I have technical skills as a Scrum Master?

Technical understanding helps you identify impediments and communicate with the team. You do not need to code, but understanding the development lifecycle is expected.
