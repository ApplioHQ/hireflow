Frequently asked questions

Do I need a license to be a security guard?

Most states require a guard card or license. List your specific license, state, and expiration date prominently on your resume.

What skills matter most for security?

Observation, communication, de-escalation, and documentation. Physical fitness matters, but employers value judgment and professionalism more.

Should I include military experience?

Absolutely. Military service demonstrates discipline, training, and the ability to follow protocols — all directly transferable to security roles.
