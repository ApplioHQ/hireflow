Frequently asked questions

What metrics should a social media manager highlight?

Follower growth, engagement rate, ROAS on paid campaigns, impressions, conversion rate, and content output volume. Show you can drive business results, not just vanity metrics.

Should I include personal social media accounts?

Only if they demonstrate relevant skills (large following, professional content). Never include personal accounts with unprofessional content.

Do I need to know paid advertising?

Increasingly, yes. Most social media manager roles expect competency in Meta Ads Manager and platform-specific ad tools. List your specific platform experience and budget managed.
