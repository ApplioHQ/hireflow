Frequently asked questions

What credentials should an SLP list?

CCC-SLP (ASHA certification), state license, and any specialty certifications (LSVT, VitalStim, PROMPT). These are required for most positions.

How do I describe caseload size?

Include number of clients/students, settings (school, outpatient, hospital), populations (pediatric, adult, geriatric), and disorder types.

Should I list specific assessment tools?

Yes. Name the standardized tests you administer (CELF, Goldman-Fristoe, MBSS) — these are keywords that ATS systems scan for.
