Frequently asked questions

How do I present substitute teaching on a resume?

Treat it like a role: list the district or agency, date range, grade levels, and subjects. Quantify assignments, schools, and request rates.

Do I need a teaching degree to substitute?

Requirements vary by state. Most require a bachelor's degree and substitute credential. List your credential and any endorsements.

Can substitute teaching lead to full-time positions?

Yes. Many districts hire from their substitute pool. Highlight long-term assignments and teacher/admin recommendations.
