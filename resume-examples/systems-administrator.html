Frequently asked questions

What certifications matter for systems administrators?

RHCSA/RHCE for Linux, MCSA/MCSE for Windows, VCP for VMware, and CompTIA Server+ or Linux+. Cloud certs (AWS/Azure) are increasingly expected.

How do I describe infrastructure at scale?

Include server count, user base supported, uptime SLAs maintained, and environment types (production, DR, dev). Scale signals seniority.

Should I list scripting skills?

Yes. PowerShell, Bash, Python, and Ansible are essential for modern sysadmin roles. Include specific automation projects.
