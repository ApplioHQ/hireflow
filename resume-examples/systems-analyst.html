Frequently asked questions

What does a systems analyst resume need?

Requirements and process work with measurable efficiency gains, plus SQL and systems-design skills.

Is this a technical or business role?

Both. Show you translate business needs into technical specs and work with engineering.

Should I list methodologies?

Yes, SDLC, Agile, and modeling notations like UML are relevant keywords.
