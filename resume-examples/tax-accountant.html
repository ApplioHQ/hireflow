Frequently asked questions

What should a tax accountant resume show?

Return volume, accuracy/penalty record, tax savings delivered, and planning or audit experience.

Do I need a CPA?

Not for all roles, but CPA or EA credentials significantly help. Mention if you're a candidate.

What software should I list?

Tax software (Lacerte, ProSeries, UltraTax), QuickBooks, and Excel.
