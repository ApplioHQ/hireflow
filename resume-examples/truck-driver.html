Frequently asked questions

What should a truck driver put on a resume?

CDL class and endorsements, total miles driven, accident-free record, types of loads hauled, and any compliance achievements. Safety record is the #1 differentiator.

Do truck drivers need a resume?

Yes. Carriers and fleet managers use resumes to assess safety record, experience level, and endorsements. A strong resume helps you get better routes and pay.

Should I list every employer?

List the last 10 years of driving employment, which is what DOT requires. Focus on the most recent and relevant positions.
