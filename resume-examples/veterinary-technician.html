Frequently asked questions

What license do vet techs need?

Most states require passing the VTNE (Veterinary Technician National Examination) and maintaining state licensure. List your credential (LVT, CVT, or RVT) prominently.

How do I stand out as a vet tech?

Highlight specific procedure experience, species handled, and any specialty skills (emergency, dentistry, exotic animals). Case volume and safety record matter.

Should I mention Fear-Free certification?

Yes. Fear-Free and Low Stress Handling certifications are increasingly valued and show commitment to patient welfare.
