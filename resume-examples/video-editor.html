Frequently asked questions

Should a video editor use a resume or a reel?

Both. The resume gets you past HR and ATS; the reel proves your creative ability. Link your reel prominently on the resume.

How do I quantify video editing work?

Videos produced, view counts, subscriber growth, turnaround times, and client retention rates all work well.

Which software should I list?

Premiere Pro is industry standard. Add DaVinci Resolve, After Effects, Final Cut Pro, and any specialized tools (Mocha, Cinema 4D) you actually use.
