Frequently asked questions

Do I need certifications for a warehouse resume?

Forklift certification is the most valuable. OSHA 10/30, first aid, and hazmat handling certifications also strengthen your resume significantly.

How do I describe warehouse work on a resume?

Lead with volume and accuracy: orders per shift, error rates, weight capacity. Use action verbs like picked, packed, loaded, audited, and coordinated.

Should I include physical abilities?

Yes. Mention lifting capacity (e.g., 50+ lbs), standing duration, and any equipment you can operate — these are genuine job requirements.
