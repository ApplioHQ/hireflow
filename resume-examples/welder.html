Frequently asked questions

What certifications should a welder list?

AWS certifications (D1.1 structural, D1.2 aluminum), ASME Section IX for pressure vessels, and any CWI/CWE credentials. List the specific processes and positions qualified.

How do I quantify welding experience?

Include weld volume, inspection pass rates, project values, and safety record. First-pass inspection rates are especially impressive.

Should I mention specific materials?

Yes. Carbon steel, stainless steel, aluminum, and exotic alloys each require different skills. Match what the job posting asks for.
