How to choose the right resume template

The best resume template depends on three things: your industry, your experience level, and whether you're applying through an ATS. Here's a quick framework:

  • Entry-level or student? Start with Harvard, Stanford, or Minimal. Clean layouts that let your education and internships stand out without needing years of experience to fill the page.
  • Tech or engineering? Jake's Resume, FAANG, or Deedy are built for one-page technical resumes with projects, skills, and quantified impact front and center.
  • Business, finance, or consulting? Wharton, Consulting, or Executive follow the structured formats MBA programs and firms expect.
  • Applying to large companies? Any template here is ATS-tested, but ATS Clean is the safest option for strict parsing systems. Single column, standard headings, zero formatting quirks.
  • Creative or design roles? Creative, Slate, or Timeline let personality show while still parsing correctly in an ATS.
