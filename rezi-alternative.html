Rezi Alternative: Applio

Updated August 2026 · Applio
Applio is a strong free alternative to Rezi for anyone who wants an ATS-optimized resume without paying up front. Like Rezi, Applio focuses on getting past Applicant Tracking Systems, but it adds a free ATS checker (no sign-up), free resume building and export, AI tailoring, and career tools that go beyond the resume.

Why choose Applio

  • Free where it counts. Build and export a real resume for free (no credit card), and check your ATS score against any job with no sign-up.
  • ATS-first. Applio scores your resume against a specific posting and lists the exact missing keywords and fixes.
  • More than a resume. AI tailoring, cover letters, interview prep, and career tools (Skill Gap, Brag Doc, First 90 Days, Salary Insights) in one place.
  • Honest AI. Applio's AI is grounded in your real experience, it never invents job titles, employers, numbers, or skills.
  • No download paywall surprises. Watermark-free export on every plan, with a one-time lifetime option if you want all AI features forever.

What Rezi does well

Rezi is a solid, focused tool if you want an ATS-first editor with real-time keyword feedback and do not mind a paid plan for full AI. Its ATS score and keyword targeting are its strengths. Applio and Rezi both aim to help you land interviews, this page is to help you pick the right fit for how you work.

Frequently asked questions

Is Applio a good free Rezi alternative?
