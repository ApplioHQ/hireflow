What affects a Marketing Manager's salary in San Francisco?

Like most metros, San Francisco salaries for Marketing Managers vary by years of experience, company size, industry, and the specific skills on your resume. Here are the biggest levers:

  • Years of experience — Senior and staff-level Marketing Managers typically earn 40–80% more than entry-level.
  • Company size — Large tech companies and enterprises pay a significant premium over startups and nonprofits in San Francisco.
  • Specialized skills — Niche technical certifications or domain expertise (e.g., cloud, ML, compliance) command above-median pay.
