# Applio Launch Video - Raylight Motion Design Brief

You are creating a 60-90 second motion design product launch/demo video for **Applio**, a free AI resume builder, using the Raylight motion design editor. This is the hero video that lives on the landing page, gets shared on social, and runs as a YouTube/TikTok ad. It needs to feel premium, fast-paced, and emotionally resonant - like an Apple or Linear product film, not a SaaS walkthrough.

---

## Brand Identity

**Product:** Applio - Free AI Resume Builder & ATS Checker
**URL:** appliohq.com
**Tagline:** "The resume that gets you interviews"
**Voice:** Confident, direct, empathetic. Not corporate, not cutesy. Talks to the job seeker like a smart friend who's been through it.

**Color palette:**
- Primary accent: #6D4DFC (electric indigo/purple)
- Secondary accent: #6366F1 (indigo)
- Success green: #22C55E
- Warning amber: #F59E0B
- Background dark: #111113
- Background card: #1A1A1D
- Surface: #232326
- Text primary: #F9FAFB
- Text muted: #9CA3AF
- Border: rgba(255,255,255,0.08)

**Typography:**
- Headlines: Inter or SF Pro Display, weight 800, tight letter-spacing (-0.02em)
- Body: Inter, weight 400-500
- Monospace accents: JetBrains Mono or SF Mono (for code/data moments)

**Visual language:**
- Glassmorphic cards with subtle backdrop blur
- Soft ambient glow behind key elements (purple halo)
- Micro-shadows, layered depth (not flat)
- Rounded corners (12-16px)
- Subtle grain/noise texture on dark backgrounds
- Motion: springy, decisive - fast ease-outs, no floaty drift

## Narrative Structure

The video follows a 5-act emotional arc. Each act has a clear beat, visual motif, and text overlay. The pacing is punchy - no shot lingers more than 3-4 seconds. Music drives the cuts.

### ACT 1: THE PROBLEM (0:00-0:15)
**Emotion:** Frustration, recognition ("this is me")
**Music:** Low, tense pulse. Muted. Building pressure.

**Beat 1 (0:00-0:04) - "The Black Hole"**
- Open on a dark void. A single resume document (white, clean, standard format) floats center-frame
- Text appears letter-by-letter in a monospace font, like a terminal: `Submitted. 47 applications.`
- The resume begins to slowly shrink/recede into the dark, as if being swallowed
- Camera: slow push in

**Beat 2 (0:04-0:08) - "The Silence"**
- Cut to a phone notification mockup on dark background
- The notification area is empty. Just the time. No responses.
- Text overlay fades in: **"No callbacks. No interviews."**
- A subtle red/amber pulse at the edges, like a warning

**Beat 3 (0:08-0:12) - "The Reason"**
- Hard cut. A resume is shown being "scanned" - a bright horizontal line sweeps down it like a barcode reader
- As the line passes, red X marks appear next to sections: wrong keywords, bad formatting, missing skills
- Counter in the corner ticks: `ATS Score: 34 / 100`
- Text: **"Your resume never reached a human."**

**Beat 4 (0:12-0:15) - "The Stat"**
- Full-frame kinetic typography, big and bold:
- `98% of Fortune 500s` (appears first, large)
- `screen with an ATS` (appears below, slightly smaller)
- `before a recruiter sees your resume.` (fades in, muted color)
- Beat drop / pause in music

### ACT 2: THE PRODUCT REVEAL (0:15-0:25)
**Emotion:** Relief, intrigue, "what is this?"
**Music:** The beat opens up. Brighter synth. Energy rising.

**Beat 5 (0:15-0:18) - "The Logo"**
- Smash cut to black
- The Applio logo (rounded square icon + wordmark) assembles from particles/fragments, snapping together with a satisfying click
- A purple glow radiates outward from behind the logo, lighting the scene
- Text beneath: **"Meet Applio."**

**Beat 6 (0:18-0:22) - "The Interface"**
- Camera pulls back to reveal the full Applio editor interface
- It's rendered as a 3D floating card, slightly rotated (5-8 degree perspective tilt), casting a soft shadow
- The interface is LIVE - cursor is moving, text is being typed, the preview pane shows a resume updating in real time
- Key UI elements glow as the camera pans across them: template picker, AI copilot panel, ATS score badge
- No text overlay here - let the product speak

**Beat 7 (0:22-0:25) - "The Score Flip"**
- Zoom into the ATS score circle
- It animates from 34 (red, from earlier) spinning up to 92 (green)
- Checkmark appears with a satisfying pop
- Text: **"From invisible to interview-ready."**

### ACT 3: THE FEATURES (0:25-0:50)
**Emotion:** Excitement, capability, "this does everything"
**Music:** Full energy. Driving rhythm. Each feature lands on a beat.

This is the montage section. Each feature gets 4-5 seconds. They should feel like rapid-fire capability reveals, each one landing with visual impact.

**Feature 1 (0:25-0:30) - "31 Templates"**
- A grid of template cards fans out across the screen in a cascading reveal (like dealing cards)
- Each card shows a different template design - Harvard, FAANG, Notion, Sidebar, Infographic visible
- The grid rotates slowly in 3D space
- One card (the user's pick) flies forward to center, enlarges, and the resume renders
- Text overlay: **"31 ATS-tested templates"**
- Subtext: "Pick a look. Switch anytime."

**Feature 2 (0:30-0:35) - "AI Writing"**
- Close-up on a resume bullet point that reads: `Responsible for managing team projects`
- A purple shimmer/wave passes over the text (the AI rewriting)
- The text transforms, letter by letter, into: `Led cross-functional team of 8, shipping 3 product launches that grew revenue 23%`
- A small "AI" badge pulses in the corner
- Text overlay: **"AI that writes like a hiring manager"**
- Subtext: "Grounded in your real experience. Never fabricates."

**Feature 3 (0:35-0:40) - "ATS Scoring"**
- Split screen: left shows a job description, right shows the resume
- Colored keyword matches light up simultaneously on both sides - green connecting lines draw between matching terms
- A live score gauge fills up: `Score: 91 / 100`
- Missing keywords are highlighted in amber with a gentle pulse
- Text overlay: **"Real ATS scoring, not a vague grade"**
- Subtext: "See exactly what to fix. Fix it in one click."

**Feature 4 (0:40-0:45) - "One-Click Tailoring"**
- A job posting card slides in from the right
- The user drags/drops it onto their resume (or clicks "Tailor")
- The resume ripples and morphs - sections reorder, keywords inject, bullets rewrite
- Before/after split wipes across the screen
- Text overlay: **"Tailored to any job in 30 seconds"**

**Feature 5 (0:45-0:50) - "The Full Toolkit"**
- Quick-cut montage of the other tools, each getting 1-1.5 seconds:
  - Cover letter generating (text flowing onto a page)
  - Interview prep (chat interface with AI coach)
  - Job tracker (kanban board with cards moving)
  - Salary calculator (bar chart animating up)
- All rendered as floating cards/screens zooming past camera
- Text overlay: **"Resume. Cover letter. Interview prep. Job tracker. All free."**

### ACT 4: THE PROOF (0:50-1:00)
**Emotion:** Trust, credibility, social proof
**Music:** Softens slightly. Still energetic but more grounded.

**Beat 13 (0:50-0:55) - "The Numbers"**
- Kinetic typography with counter animations:
  - `250+` job seekers (counter ticks up rapidly)
  - `31` ATS templates
  - `11` free AI tools
  - `0-100` live ATS score
- Each number pops in with a scale-up animation, landing on beat
- Numbers are large, bold, white with purple accent on the metric labels

**Beat 14 (0:55-1:00) - "The Difference"**
- A simple, elegant comparison:
- Left side: generic resume, grayed out, X marks, `Score: 34` in red
- Right side: Applio resume, vibrant, check marks, `Score: 92` in green, purple glow
- The left fades away, the right expands to fill frame
- Text: **"Same experience. Different outcome."**

### ACT 5: THE CTA (1:00-1:10)
**Emotion:** Urgency, empowerment, "I'm doing this right now"
**Music:** Final build to crescendo, then clean resolve.

**Beat 15 (1:00-1:05) - "The Promise"**
- Center frame, large text animation (word by word, each word appearing with a spring bounce):
- **"Build the resume"**
- **"that gets you"**
- **"interviews."**
- Purple underline sweeps under "interviews" with a glow

**Beat 16 (1:05-1:10) - "The Button"**
- The CTA button from the website materializes center-frame: `Build My Resume Free ->`
- It's large, purple (#6D4DFC), with a subtle shine animation moving across it
- Below the button: `Free forever. No credit card. Your data never sold.`
- The Applio logo and wordmark sit above
- URL appears: **appliohq.com**
- Hold for 3 seconds (the social share / screenshot moment)

## Motion Design Principles

**Timing:**
- All eases: cubic-bezier(0.16, 1, 0.3, 1) - fast out, gentle settle (spring feel)
- Text reveals: 200-300ms per word, staggered 50ms
- Card transitions: 400-600ms
- Score counters: 800ms with overshoot
- Cuts happen ON the beat, not between beats

**Camera:**
- Subtle constant drift (0.5-1 degree) to keep the frame alive
- Push-ins for emphasis, pull-backs for reveals
- Never static for more than 2 seconds
- Depth of field: shallow, with foreground blur on establishing shots

**Particles & Effects:**
- Subtle floating particle dust in the background (very low opacity, slow drift)
- Purple ambient glow behind hero elements
- Glassmorphic blur on overlapping cards
- Grain/noise: 2-4% on dark backgrounds for texture
- Light leaks on transitions (very subtle, purple-tinted)

**Text animation:**
- Headlines: mask reveal (slide up from behind a line) or spring scale-in
- Body text: fade + slight Y translate (12px up)
- Numbers/counters: ticker animation (digits cycling)
- Monospace text: typewriter effect with blinking cursor

**Transitions between acts:**
- Act 1 -> 2: Hard smash cut to black (the product reveal moment)
- Act 2 -> 3: Smooth zoom into a UI element that becomes the first feature
- Act 3 -> 4: Quick dissolve with a purple flash
- Act 4 -> 5: Elegant fade through black

## Key Creative Constraints

1. **No stock footage.** Everything is motion graphics, UI mockups, and kinetic typography.
2. **No human faces or hands.** This is a product film, not a testimonial.
3. **The product IS the hero.** Every frame should make Applio look premium and capable.
4. **Speed over explanation.** This is a highlight reel, not a tutorial. Each feature is a flash of capability, not a walkthrough.
5. **Dark theme dominant.** 90% dark backgrounds. Light elements are the resume cards and text.
6. **Purple is the hero color.** It should feel like the purple glow is the "AI energy" powering everything.
7. **End frame must be screenshot-worthy.** The final CTA frame should work as a thumbnail and still image.
8. **Accessible:** Ensure text contrast meets WCAG AA on every frame. No fast strobing. Captions for any voiceover (if added later).
