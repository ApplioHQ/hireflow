How to use these tools together

Each tool solves one part of the resume process. Here's the order that gets the best results:

  1. Score your current resume with the Resume Scorer to see where you stand across content strength, action verbs, quantified impact, and completeness.
  2. Fix weak bullets using the Bullet Generator. Turn vague duties ("responsible for managing...") into quantified impact statements.
  3. Write your summary with the Summary Generator. A strong 2-3 sentence summary hooks recruiters in the first five seconds.
  4. Tailor for each job using the Tailoring Tool. Paste the job description and the AI rewrites your bullets to mirror the posting's language.
  5. Run the ATS check with the ATS Checker before you submit. See matched and missing keywords, and fix formatting issues that break parsing.

Why these tools exist

Most resume builders give you a template and leave you staring at blank fields. The hard part was never picking a font, it's writing bullet points that sound professional, choosing the right keywords for ATS, and tailoring your resume without spending 45 minutes per application.

Frequently asked questions

Are these resume tools really free?

Most tools are completely free with no sign-up: ATS checker, resume scorer, analyzer, job match, proofreader, bullet examples, and the template quiz. AI-powered tools (bullet generator, summary generator, skills generator, tailoring, cover letter writer) use the free trial or Premium.

How does the ATS checker work?

Paste your resume text and a job description. The checker compares keyword overlap, section structure, and formatting to give you a compatibility score and a list of missing keywords to add before you apply.

Can AI write my resume for me?

Applio's AI writes bullet points, professional summaries, and skills sections from a few guided questions. It uses your real experience as input, no buzzwords like "spearheaded" or "leveraged" that hiring managers flag as AI-generated.

How is AI tailoring different from rewriting myself?

The AI reads the job description and mirrors its specific language into your existing bullets. It doesn't fabricate experience. The result reads like you wrote it for that exact role, in seconds instead of 45 minutes.

What data do you collect from my resume?

Free browser-based tools (ATS checker, scorer, analyzer) process your text locally, nothing is stored. AI tools send your text to our API for processing but do not store resume content after the response is generated.
